> [!thm] [[Theorem 1 (First Theorem Of Graph Theory)]]: Let $G$ be a graph. Then $$\sum_{v\in V(G)}d(v)=2m(G).$$

> [!proof]
> Count vertex-edge incidences in two ways. By [[Definition 9 (Degree And Neighborhood)]], counting by vertices gives $\sum_{v\in V(G)}d(v)$. Every edge has two ends, so counting by edges gives $2m(G)$. $\blacksquare$

> [!cor] [[Corollary 1]]: Every graph has an even number of vertices of odd degree.

> [!proof]
> If the number of odd-degree vertices were odd, the degree sum would be odd, contradiction.

> [!cor] [[Corollary 2]]: The average degree of a graph satisfies $\delta(G) \leq \frac{2m}{n} \leq \Delta(G).$

> [!cor] [[Corollary 3]]: A $k$-regular graph with order $n$ has size $m = \frac{nk}{2}.$
