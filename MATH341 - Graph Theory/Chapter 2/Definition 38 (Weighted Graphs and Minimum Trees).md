
> [!def] [Definition 38 (Weighted Graphs and Minimum Trees)](Definition%2038%20%28Weighted%20Graphs%20and%20Minimum%20Trees%29.md): A **weighted graph** assigns a nonnegative **weight** $w(e)$ to every edge $e$. A **minimum spanning tree** is a spanning tree whose total edge weight is as small as possible.

###### Motivation.
A set of villages can be modeled by vertices and potential electrical connections by edges. Edge weights represent costs that depend on terrain, distance, and land value. A minimum spanning tree connects all villages at minimum total cost.
