
> [!lem] [Lemma 05 (Adding a Vertex Preserves Connectivity)](Lemma%2005%20%28Adding%20a%20Vertex%20Preserves%20Connectivity%29.md): Adjoining a new vertex adjacent to $k$ vertices of a $k$-connected graph preserves $k$-connectivity.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Delete fewer than $k$ vertices from the augmented graph. The surviving part of the original graph is connected. If the new vertex remains, it has a surviving neighbor because fewer than $k$ vertices were deleted. Thus the surviving graph is connected in either case. $\blacksquare$
