
> [!def] [Definition 48 (Line Graphs and Digraphs)](Definition%2048%20%28Line%20Graphs%20and%20Digraphs%29.md): The **line graph** $L(G)$ has one vertex for each edge of $G$, with two distinct vertices adjacent exactly when the original edges share an endpoint. The **line digraph** has one vertex for each arc and an arc from $e$ to $f$ when the head of $e$ is the tail of $f$.

###### Motivation.
The construction turns an edge problem into a vertex problem. In particular, it connects the two forms of Menger's theorem.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: $L(K_{1,4})=K_{4}$. The line graph of $K_{4}$ is the octahedral graph $K_{2,2,2}$.
