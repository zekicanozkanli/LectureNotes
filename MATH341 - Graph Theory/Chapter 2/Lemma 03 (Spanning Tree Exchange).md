
> [!lem] [Lemma 03 (Spanning Tree Exchange)](Lemma%2003%20%28Spanning%20Tree%20Exchange%29.md): If $T$ and $T'$ are spanning trees of a connected graph and $e\in E(T)\setminus E(T')$, then there is an edge $e'\in E(T')\setminus E(T)$ such that both $T-e+e'$ and $T'+e-e'$ are spanning trees.
