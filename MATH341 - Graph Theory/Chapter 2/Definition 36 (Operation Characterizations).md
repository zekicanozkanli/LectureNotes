
> [!def] [Definition 36 (Operation Characterizations)](Definition%2036%20%28Operation%20Characterizations%29.md): An **operation characterization** of a graph class specifies starting graphs and operations that stay within the class and generate every graph in it.

###### Motivation.
To prove a property for the whole class, prove it for each starting graph and show that each permitted operation preserves it. A graph may have several construction sequences.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The class of trees is characterized by starting from $K_{1}$ and adding leaves, as in [Theorem 05 (Constructing Trees)](Theorem%2005%20%28Constructing%20Trees%29.md).
