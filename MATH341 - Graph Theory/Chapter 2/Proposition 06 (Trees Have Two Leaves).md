
> [!prop] [Proposition 06 (Trees Have Two Leaves)](Proposition%2006%20%28Trees%20Have%20Two%20Leaves%29.md): Every nontrivial tree has at least two leaves.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Let $P$ be a maximal path in the tree. Each endpoint has exactly one neighbor on $P$, since any additional neighbor on $P$ gives a cycle. An endpoint cannot have a neighbor outside $P$, since that would extend the path. Both endpoints therefore have degree $1$. $\blacksquare$
