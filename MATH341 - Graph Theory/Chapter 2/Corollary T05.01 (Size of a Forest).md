
> [!cor] [Corollary T05.01 (Size of a Forest)](Corollary%20T05.01%20%28Size%20of%20a%20Forest%29.md): A tree of order $n$ has $n-1$ edges. A forest of order $n$ with $k$ components has $n-k$ edges.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* The construction in [Theorem 05 (Constructing Trees)](Theorem%2005%20%28Constructing%20Trees%29.md) starts with one vertex and no edges, and each step adds one vertex and one edge. For a forest, sum $n_{i}-1$ over the $k$ component orders $n_{1},\ldots,n_{k}$. $\blacksquare$
