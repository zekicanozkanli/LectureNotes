
> [!def] [Definition 37 (Spanning Trees and Unicyclic Graphs)](Definition%2037%20%28Spanning%20Trees%20and%20Unicyclic%20Graphs%29.md): A **spanning tree** of a connected graph $G$ is a tree subgraph with vertex set $V(G)$. A **unicyclic graph** is a connected graph with exactly one cycle.

> [!rem] Remark. Deleting an edge of a cycle preserves connectivity. Repeating this until no cycle remains gives a spanning tree. A tree plus one extra edge is unicyclic, and a unicyclic graph becomes a tree when an edge of its unique cycle is deleted.
