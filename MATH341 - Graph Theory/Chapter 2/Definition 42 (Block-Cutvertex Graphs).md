
> [!def] [Definition 42 (Block-Cutvertex Graphs)](Definition%2042%20%28Block-Cutvertex%20Graphs%29.md): The **block-cutvertex graph** of $G$ is bipartite, with the blocks of $G$ as one part and its cut-vertices as the other. A block is adjacent to each cut-vertex it contains.

Remark. Her bir blok bir vertex ile temsil ediliyor.
###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The block-cutvertex graph of the example in [Definition 41 (Cut-Vertices Bridges and Blocks)](Definition%2041%20%28Cut-Vertices%20Bridges%20and%20Blocks%29.md) is $P_{7}$.
