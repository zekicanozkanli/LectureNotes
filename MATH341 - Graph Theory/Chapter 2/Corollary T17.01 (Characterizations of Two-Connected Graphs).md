
> [!cor] [Corollary T17.01 (Characterizations of Two-Connected Graphs)](Corollary%20T17.01%20%28Characterizations%20of%20Two-Connected%20Graphs%29.md): For a graph of order at least three, the following are equivalent.  
> (i) The graph is $2$-connected.  
> (ii) Every pair of distinct vertices is joined by two independent paths.  
> (iii) Every pair of distinct vertices lies on a common cycle.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* (i) and (ii) are equivalent by [Theorem 17 (Global Forms of Menger’s Theorem)](Theorem%2017%20%28Global%20Forms%20of%20Menger%E2%80%99s%20Theorem%29.md). The union of two independent paths with the same endpoints is a cycle, and the two arcs of a cycle between distinct specified vertices are independent paths. Thus (ii) and (iii) are equivalent. $\blacksquare$
