
> [!def] [Definition 40 (Rooted Trees)](Definition%2040%20%28Rooted%20Trees%29.md): A **rooted tree** is a tree with a distinguished vertex, its **root**.

###### Motivation.
The root is the starting point in a shortest-path tree. Rooted trees serve as data structures for efficient retrieval, and joining another tree at its root is useful for recursive constructions.
