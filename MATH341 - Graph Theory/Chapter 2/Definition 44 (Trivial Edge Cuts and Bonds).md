
> [!def] [Definition 44 (Trivial Edge Cuts and Bonds)](Definition%2044%20%28Trivial%20Edge%20Cuts%20and%20Bonds%29.md): A **trivial edge cut** consists entirely of edges incident to a single vertex. A **bond** is a nonempty edge cut that is minimal under inclusion.
