
> [!prop] [Proposition 11 (Bonds Split Two Components)](Proposition%2011%20%28Bonds%20Split%20Two%20Components%29.md): If $X$ is a bond of a connected graph $G$, then $G-X$ has exactly two components.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* There are at least two components. If there were at least three, choose an edge $e$ of $X$ joining two of them. In $G-(X\setminus\{e\})$, the restored edge merges at most two components, leaving the graph disconnected. The edges across one of its components give an edge cut properly contained in $X$, contradicting minimality. $\blacksquare$
