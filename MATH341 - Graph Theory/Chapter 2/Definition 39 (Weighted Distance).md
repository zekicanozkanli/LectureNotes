
> [!def] [Definition 39 (Weighted Distance)](Definition%2039%20%28Weighted%20Distance%29.md): The **distance** $d(u,z)$ in a weighted graph is the least total edge weight of a $u$–$z$ path, or $\infty$ if there is no such path.

> [!rem] Remark. Each initial segment of a shortest path is itself shortest to its endpoint. The unweighted distance corresponds to assigning weight $1$ to every edge.

###### Motivation.
Route-finding can minimize geographical distance, travel time, or monetary cost by assigning the corresponding edge weights.
