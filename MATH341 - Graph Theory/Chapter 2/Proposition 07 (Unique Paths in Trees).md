
> [!prop] [Proposition 07 (Unique Paths in Trees)](Proposition%2007%20%28Unique%20Paths%20in%20Trees%29.md): A graph is a tree if and only if each pair of vertices is joined by a unique path.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* ($\Rightarrow$) Connectivity gives a path. If two paths with the same endpoints differ, their first divergence and subsequent reunion give a cycle.
>
> ($\Leftarrow$) Path existence gives connectivity. Any cycle would give two paths between distinct vertices on it. $\blacksquare$
