
> [!def] [Definition 49 (Edge Separators and Independent Paths)](Definition%2049%20%28Edge%20Separators%20and%20Independent%20Paths%29.md): An **$uv$-edge cut** in a directed multigraph is a set of arcs whose deletion destroys every $u$–$v$ path. A family of such paths is **edge independent** if its members are pairwise edge-disjoint.
