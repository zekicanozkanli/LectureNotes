> [!prop] [[Proposition 2 (Eight-Cycles In The Petersen Graph)]]: The Petersen graph contains $15$ $8$-cycles.

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Any 8-cycle omits two vertices. They must be adjacent, or else they would have a common neighbor that could not be on the cycle. Deleting two adjacent vertices from the Petersen graph results in a graph that clearly contains a single 8-cycle. The Petersen graph has 15 edges and, hence, 15 pairs of adjacent vertices, so it has 15 8-cycles.
>
