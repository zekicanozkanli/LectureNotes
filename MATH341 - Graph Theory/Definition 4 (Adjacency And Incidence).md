> [!def] [[Definition 4 (Adjacency And Incidence)]]: If $e=uv$ and $f=uw$ are edges of a graph, then we write $u\leftrightarrow v$ and say $u$ and $v$ are adjacent, or neighbors. Moreover, we say $e$ joins $u$ and $v$; $u$ is joined to $v$; and $e$ and $v$ are incident.
