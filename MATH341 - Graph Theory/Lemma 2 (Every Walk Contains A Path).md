> [!lem] [[Lemma 2 (Every Walk Contains A Path)]]: Every $u-v$ walk contains a $u-v$ path.


###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Assume to the contrary that there is a $u − v$ walk that does not contain a $u − v$ path, and let $W$ be such a walk with minimum length. Then $W$ must repeat some vertex $w$. Deleting all the vertices and edges on the walk between the occurrences of $w$ produces a shorter $u − v$ walk. Either it is a $u − v$ path or it is a smaller counterexample. Either way, there is a contradiction.
