> [!def] [[Definition 19 (Girth)]]: The girth of a graph is the length of its shortest cycle.

###### Examples.

i. The Petersen graph has girth $5$: it contains a $5$-cycle and has neither triangles nor $4$-cycles.

###### Bibliography.

[1] Bickle. (n.d.). *Fundamentals of Graph Theory* (Chapter 1, p. 11). Supplied excerpt.
