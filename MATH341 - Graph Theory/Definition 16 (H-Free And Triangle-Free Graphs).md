> [!def] [[Definition 16 (H-Free And Triangle-Free Graphs)]]: A graph $G$ is $H$-free if it contains no induced subgraph isomorphic to $H$.
###### Examples.
$K_3$ is a triangle; a $K_3$-free graph is triangle-free.
###### Bibliography.
[1] Bickle. (n.d.). *Fundamentals of Graph Theory* (Chapter 1, p. 10). Supplied excerpt.
