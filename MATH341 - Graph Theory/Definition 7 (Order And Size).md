> [!def] [[Definition 7 (Order And Size)]]: The order $n(G)=|V(G)|$ of a graph $G$ is its number of vertices. The size $m(G)=|E(G)|$ is its number of edges. When it is clear, we write $n$ and $m$, respectively.
