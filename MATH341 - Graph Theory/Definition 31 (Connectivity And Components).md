> [!def] [[Definition 31 (Connectivity And Components)]]: A graph is connected if it contains a $u-v$ path for every pair of vertices $u,v$; it is disconnected otherwise. A component is a maximal connected subgraph.

A disconnected graph is the disjoint union of its components. Its size is the sum of their sizes; its maximum degree is the maximum of their maximum degrees.

###### Examples.

i. The components of $C_4\cup P_3$ are $C_4$ and $P_3$.
