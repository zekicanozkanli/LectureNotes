> [!prop] [[Proposition 5 (Diameter Of The Complement)]]: If $\operatorname{diam}(G)\ge4$, then $\operatorname{diam}(\overline G)\le2$.
###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Let $diam (G) ≥ 4$, and let $x$ and $y$ be vertices with $d(x, y) = 4$. If $u$ and $v$ are nonadjacent in $G$, then they are adjacent in $G$. If $u$ and $v$ are adjacent in $G$, then $x$ and $y$ cannot both be in the neighborhoods of $u$ and $v$, since that would imply an $x − y$ path of length at most 3. Thus $u$ and $v$ are both nonadjacent to either $x$ or $y$. Then $d(u, v) = 2 \in G$.
