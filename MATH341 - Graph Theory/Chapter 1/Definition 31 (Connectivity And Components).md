
> [!def] [[Definition 31 (Connectivity And Components)]]: A graph is **connected** if it contains a $u-v$ path for every pair of vertices $u,v$. A graph that is not connected is called **disconnected**. A **component** is a maximal connected subgraph.
###### Examples.

i. The components of $C_4\cup P_3$ are $C_4$ and $P_3$.
