
> [!prop] [[Proposition 4 (Radius And Diameter Bounds)]]: For every connected graph, $$\operatorname{rad}(G)\le\operatorname{diam}(G)\le2\operatorname{rad}(G).$$
###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> The first inequality follows from [[Definition 32 (Distance, Geodesic, Diameter, Radius, Eccentricity and Center)]]. Let $u$ and $v$ be vertices with $d(u, v) = diam(G)$, and let $w$ be a vertex with $ecc(w) = rad (G)$. By the triangle inequality, $$diam(G)=d(u,v)\leq d(u,w)+d(w,v) \leq 2ecc(v)=2rad (G).$$
