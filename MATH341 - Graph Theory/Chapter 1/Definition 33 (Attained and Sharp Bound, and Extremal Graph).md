
> [!def] [[Definition 33 (Attained and Sharp Bound, and Extremal Graph)]]): Any bound is called **attained** if some graph gives equality, and is called **sharp** if infinitely many graphs give equality. Graphs giving equality are extremal graphs for the bound.

###### Examples.

i. For $P_5$, we have $diam(P_5)=4=2rad(P_5).$ Hence, $P_5$ attains the bound under the [[Proposition 4 (Radius And Diameter Bounds)]] and is an extremal graph. For $P_{2k+1}$, $rad(P_{2k+1})=k$ and $diam(P_{2k+1})=2k$. Thus the upper bound is sharp.
