
> [!def] [[Definition 22 (Complement And Decomposition)]]: The complement $\overline G$ of a graph $G$ has the same vertex set $V$ as $G$, and for distinct $u, v \in V$, $$ \{u, v\} \in E(\overline G) \iff \{u, v\} \notin E(G).$$ A decomposition of $G$ is a set of nonempty subgraphs whose edge sets partition $E(G)$.

Note that an empty graph is the complement of a complete graph of the same order. A graph $G$ of order $n$ and its complement decompose $K_n$.
###### Examples.

i. $\overline{C_4}=2K_2$, so $\{C_4,2K_2\}$ is a decomposition of $K_4$.
