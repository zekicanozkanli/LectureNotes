
> [!def] [[Definition 25 (Complete Bipartite Graphs And Stars)]]: The complete bipartite graph $K_{r;s}$ is the join of two empty graphs: $K_{r;s}=\overline K_r+\overline K_s$. A star is $K_{1;s}$. 
###### Examples.

i. $K_{1;3}$ and $K_{3;3}$. The star $K_{1;3}$ is called a claw when it occurs as an induced subgraph.
