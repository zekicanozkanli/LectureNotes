
> [!def] [[Definition 34 (Bipartite Graphs And Matchings)]]: A graph is bipartite if its vertex set can be partitioned into two independent sets. A matching is a set of edges, no two of which are adjacent. A perfect matching has an edge incident with every vertex.
###### Examples.

i. For instance, consider $P_5$. Take $A=\{1, 3, 5\}, \text{ and } B=\{2, 4\}.$ This construction shows $P_5$ is bipartite.
ii. Paths, even cycles, grids, and hypercubes are bipartite.
