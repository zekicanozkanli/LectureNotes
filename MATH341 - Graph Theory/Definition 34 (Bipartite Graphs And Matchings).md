> [!def] [[Definition 34 (Bipartite Graphs And Matchings)]]: A graph is bipartite if its vertex set can be partitioned into two independent sets, called partite sets. A matching is a set of edges, no two of which are adjacent. A perfect matching has an edge incident with every vertex.


A bipartite graph is also called $2$-colorable: color the two partite sets differently so that adjacent vertices have different colors. A complete bipartite graph has all possible edges between its partite sets.

###### Examples.

i. Paths, even cycles, grids, and hypercubes are bipartite.
