> [!def] [[Definition 9 (Degree And Neighborhood)]]: The degree of a vertex $v$, written $d_G(v)$ or $d(v)$, is the number of edges incident with $v$. A vertex of degree $0$ is called isolated and a vertex of degree $1$ is a leaf. An even vertex has even degree; an odd vertex has odd degree. The neighborhood $N(v)$ of a vertex $v$ is the set of neighbors of $v$.

Note that $d(v)$ is the number of $1$ entries in its adjacency-matrix row or column.
