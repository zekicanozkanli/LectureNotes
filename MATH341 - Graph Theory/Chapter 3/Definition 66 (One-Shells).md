
> [!def] [Definition 66 (One-Shells)](Definition%2066%20%28One-Shells%29.md): The **$1$-shell** of a graph is the subgraph formed by edges outside its $2$-core and the endpoints of those edges.

> [!rem] Remark. If nonempty, the $1$-shell is a forest with no isolated vertices and with at most one vertex of the $2$-core in each component. A cycle outside the core, or a path outside it joining two core vertices, would extend a subgraph of minimum degree at least two.
