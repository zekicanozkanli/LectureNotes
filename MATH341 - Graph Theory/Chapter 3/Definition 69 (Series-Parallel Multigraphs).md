
> [!def] [Definition 69 (Series-Parallel Multigraphs)](Definition%2069%20%28Series-Parallel%20Multigraphs%29.md): A **series-parallel multigraph** is built from $K_{2}$ by repeatedly subdividing edges or duplicating edges. The two original vertices can serve as its source and sink.

###### Motivation.
Series and parallel compositions model electrical networks. Resistances add in series, $R=R_{1}+R_{2}$. In parallel, their reciprocals add, $1/R=1/R_{1}+1/R_{2}$.
