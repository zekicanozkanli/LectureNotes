
> [!cor] [Corollary T28.01 (An Edge Bound for Cores)](Corollary%20T28.01%20%28An%20Edge%20Bound%20for%20Cores%29.md): Let $1\leq k\leq n-1$. A graph of order $n$ and size at least $(k-1)n-\binom{k}{2}+1$ has a $k$-core.
