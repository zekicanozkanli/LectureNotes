
> [!ex] [Exercise 34 (Extracting Paths from Trails)](Exercise%2034%20%28Extracting%20Paths%20from%20Trails%29.md): Show that every $u-v$ trail contains a $u-v$ path.

**What to show.** Find a path with endpoints $u$ and $v$ that uses only edges of the given trail and repeats no vertex.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* A $u-v$ trail is a $u-v$ walk. By [Lemma 2 (Every Walk Contains A Path)](../../Chapter%201/Lemma%202%20%28Every%20Walk%20Contains%20A%20Path%29.md), it contains a $u-v$ path. $\blacksquare$
