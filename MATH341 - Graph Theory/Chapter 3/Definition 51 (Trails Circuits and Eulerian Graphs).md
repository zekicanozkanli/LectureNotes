
> [!def] [Definition 51 (Trails Circuits and Eulerian Graphs)](Definition%2051%20%28Trails%20Circuits%20and%20Eulerian%20Graphs%29.md): A **trail** is a walk that repeats no edge. A **closed trail**, or **circuit**, has equal endpoints, and an **open trail** has distinct endpoints. An **Eulerian circuit** uses every edge of its graph. An **Eulerian trail** uses every edge and is open. An **Eulerian graph** has an Eulerian circuit.

###### Motivation.
Euler modeled the seven bridges of Königsberg by a multigraph with one vertex per landmass and one edge per bridge. A closed walk crossing each bridge exactly once would pair the entering and leaving edges at each vertex. All four vertices had odd degree, making the proposed walk impossible. Euler's analysis of the problem in 1736 is commonly regarded as the beginning of graph theory.
