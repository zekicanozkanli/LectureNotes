
> [!def] [Definition 67 (K-Trees)](Definition%2067%20%28K-Trees%29.md): A **$k$-tree** is built from $K_{k+1}$ by repeatedly adjoining a vertex whose neighbors among the existing vertices are exactly the vertices of a $k$-clique. The initial complete graph is the **root** of the construction.

> [!rem] Remark. Every $k$-tree is maximal $k$-degenerate. The $1$-trees are the nontrivial trees under this starting convention. For $k\geq2$, the class of maximal $k$-degenerate graphs is larger. A construction may also begin at any specified $k$-clique by taking that clique first and adding its first common neighbor. Identifying a $k$-clique in one $k$-tree with a $k$-clique in another gives a $k$-tree.
