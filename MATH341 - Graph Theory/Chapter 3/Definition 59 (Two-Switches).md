
> [!def] [Definition 59 (Two-Switches)](Definition%2059%20%28Two-Switches%29.md): A **2-switch** on four distinct vertices $u,v,w,x$ deletes existing edges $uv$ and $wx$ and adds previously absent edges $uw$ and $vx$.

> [!rem] Remark. The operation preserves the degree of each vertex. It is the exchange used in the proof of [Theorem 23 (Havel–Hakimi Theorem)](Theorem%2023%20%28Havel%E2%80%93Hakimi%20Theorem%29.md).
