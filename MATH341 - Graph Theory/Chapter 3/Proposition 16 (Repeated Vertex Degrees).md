
> [!prop] [Proposition 16 (Repeated Vertex Degrees)](Proposition%2016%20%28Repeated%20Vertex%20Degrees%29.md): Every graph of order at least two has two vertices of equal degree.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* The possible degrees run from $0$ to $n-1$. A vertex of degree $n-1$ rules out any vertex of degree $0$, so at most $n-1$ distinct degree values occur among the $n$ vertices. $\blacksquare$
