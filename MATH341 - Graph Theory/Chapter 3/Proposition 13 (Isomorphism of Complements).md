
> [!prop] [Proposition 13 (Isomorphism of Complements)](Proposition%2013%20%28Isomorphism%20of%20Complements%29.md): For graphs $G,H$, one has $G\cong H$ if and only if $\overline{G}\cong\overline{H}$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The complement of a cubic graph on six vertices is $2$-regular, hence is either $2C_{3}$ or $C_{6}$. Complementing these two yields $K_{3,3}$ and $K_{3}\mathbin{\square}K_{2}$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* An isomorphism matches edges and nonedges, so the same map is an isomorphism of the complements. Applying the argument to the complements gives the converse. $\blacksquare$
