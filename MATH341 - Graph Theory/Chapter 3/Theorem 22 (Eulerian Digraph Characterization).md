
> [!thm] [Theorem 22 (Eulerian Digraph Characterization)](Theorem%2022%20%28Eulerian%20Digraph%20Characterization%29.md): A weakly connected digraph is Eulerian if and only if every vertex has equal outdegree and indegree.
