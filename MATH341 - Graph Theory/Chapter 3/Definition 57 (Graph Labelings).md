
> [!def] [Definition 57 (Graph Labelings)](Definition%2057%20%28Graph%20Labelings%29.md): A **labeling** is an assignment $f:V(G)\to\mathbb{R}$ of vertex labels. When assigning distinct labels from $[n]$, two assignments are counted as distinct labelings if they produce different labeled edge sets.
