
> [!def] [Definition 68 (Lobes of Vertex Cuts)](Definition%2068%20%28Lobes%20of%20Vertex%20Cuts%29.md): For a vertex cut $S$, an **$S$-lobe** is the subgraph induced by $S$ together with one component of $G-S$.
