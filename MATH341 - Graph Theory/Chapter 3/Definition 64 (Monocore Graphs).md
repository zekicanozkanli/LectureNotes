
> [!def] [Definition 64 (Monocore Graphs)](Definition%2064%20%28Monocore%20Graphs%29.md): A graph is **$k$-monocore** if $D(G)=\delta(G)=k$. It is **monocore** if this holds for some $k$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The $0$-monocore graphs are the edgeless graphs. The connected $1$-monocore graphs are the nontrivial trees. Every regular graph is monocore.
