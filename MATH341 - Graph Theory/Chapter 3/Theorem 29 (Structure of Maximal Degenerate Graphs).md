
> [!thm] [Theorem 29 (Structure of Maximal Degenerate Graphs)](Theorem%2029%20%28Structure%20of%20Maximal%20Degenerate%20Graphs%29.md): Let $G$ be maximal $k$-degenerate of order $n$, with $1\leq k\leq n-1$.
>
> (a) It contains a $(k+1)$-clique. For $n\geq k+2$ it contains $K_{k+2}-e$ as a subgraph.  
> (b) For $n\geq k+2$, its minimum degree is $k$, and no two vertices of degree $k$ are adjacent.  
> (c) Its connectivity is $k$.  
> (d) For every $1\leq r\leq n$, it has an induced maximal $k$-degenerate graph of order $r$. In the vertex-addition test, a graph with $n\geq k+2$ and a vertex $v$ of degree $k$ is maximal $k$-degenerate exactly when $G-v$ is.  
> (e) The maximal $1$-degenerate graphs are trees.

> [!rem] Remark. In particular, maximal $k$-degenerate graphs are monocore.
