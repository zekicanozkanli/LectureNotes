> [!def] [[Definition 33 (Attained And Sharp Bounds)]]: A bound is **attained** if some graph gives equality. A bound is sharp if infinitely many graphs give equality. Graphs giving equality are extremal graphs for the bound.

###### Examples.

i. For $P_{2k+1}$, $operatorname{rad}(P_{2k+1})=k$ and $operatorname{diam}(P_{2k+1})=2k$. Hence the upper bound in [[Proposition 4 (Radius And Diameter Bounds)]] is sharp.
