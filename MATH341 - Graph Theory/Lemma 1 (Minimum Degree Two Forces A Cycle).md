> [!lem] [[Lemma 1 (Minimum Degree Two Forces A Cycle)]]: If a graph $G$ has $\delta(G)\ge2$, then it contains a cycle.
###### Proof.

> [!proof]
>Let $P$ be a maximal path in $G$ with vertices $v_1,\dots,v_n$. $v_n$ has degree at least $2$, so it is adjacent to some vertex $u \neq v_{n-1}$. If $u$ is not on the path, it could be extended, contradicting its maximality. Thus $u$ must be on the path, forming a cycle.

[1] Bickle. (n.d.). *Fundamentals of Graph Theory* (Chapter 1, p. 10). Supplied excerpt.
