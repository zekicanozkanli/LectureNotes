> [!def] [[Definition 29 (Walks And Path Length)]]: A walk is a list $v_0,e_1,v_1,\dots,e_k,v_k$ of vertices and edges such that $e_i=v_{i-1}v_i$. A $u-v$ walk has endpoints $u=v_0$ and $v=v_k$. It is closed if $v_0=v_k.$ A $u-v$ path is a path with endpoints $u$ and $v$; its other vertices are called internal vertices. The length of a walk, path, or cycle is its number of edges.

Note that a path is a walk with no repeated vertices.
