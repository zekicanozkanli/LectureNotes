
> [!thm] [Theorem 37 (Continuity into Product Spaces)](Theorem%2037%20%28Continuity%20into%20Product%20Spaces%29.md): A function $f:Z\to X\times Y$ into a product space is continuous if and only if both $p_1\circ f:Z\to X$ and $p_2\circ f:Z\to Y$ are continuous.
