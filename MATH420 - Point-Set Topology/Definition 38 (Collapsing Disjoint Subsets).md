
> [!def] [Definition 38 (Collapsing Disjoint Subsets)](Definition%2038%20%28Collapsing%20Disjoint%20Subsets%29.md): Let $(X,\mathcal{T})$ be a topological space and let $Y_1,\ldots,Y_r\subseteq X$ satisfy:  
> i. $Y_i\ne\emptyset$ for every $i$.  
> ii. $Y_i\cap Y_j=\emptyset$ whenever $i\ne j$.  
> Define $x\sim y$ if and only if $x=y$ or $x,y\in Y_i$ for some $i\in\{1,\ldots,r\}$. The resulting quotient space is denoted $X/Y_1,Y_2,\ldots,Y_r$; each $Y_i$ is **collapsed to a point**.
