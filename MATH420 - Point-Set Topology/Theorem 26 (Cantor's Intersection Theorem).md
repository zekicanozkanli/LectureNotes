
> [!thm] [Theorem 26 (Cantor's Intersection Theorem)](Theorem%2026%20%28Cantor%27s%20Intersection%20Theorem%29.md): A metric space $(X,d)$ is complete if and only if every decreasing sequence $(S_n)$ of nonempty closed subsets with $\operatorname{diam}(S_n)=\sup\{d(x,y)\mid x,y\in S_n\}\to0$ has nonempty intersection $\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}S_n$. In fact, the intersection is a singleton.

> [!rem] The quantity $\operatorname{diam}(S_n)$ is the diameter of $S_n$. No proof is supplied.
