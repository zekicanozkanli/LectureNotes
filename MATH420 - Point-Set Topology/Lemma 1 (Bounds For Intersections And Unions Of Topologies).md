
> [!prop] [[Lemma 1 (Bounds For Intersections And Unions Of Topologies)]]: Let $X\ne\emptyset$ and $\{\mathcal T_i\}_{i\in I}$ be a collection of topologies on $X$. Then
> i. $\bigcap_{i\in I}\mathcal T_i$ is coarser than each $\mathcal T_i$,
> ii. if $\bigcup_{i\in I}\mathcal T_i$ is a topology on $X$, then it is finer than for each $\mathcal T_i$.
