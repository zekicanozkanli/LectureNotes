# Set Operations

All sets below are subsets of a set $X$.

i. The union of two sets $A$ and $B$ is
$$A\cup B=\{x\mid x\in A\text{ or }x\in B\}.$$

ii. The intersection of two sets $A$ and $B$ is
$$A\cap B=\{x\mid x\in A\text{ and }x\in B\}.$$

iii. The relative complement of a set $B$ with respect to a set $A$, or simply the difference of $A$ and $B$, is
$$A\setminus B=A\cap(X\setminus B)=\{x\mid x\in A\text{ and }x\notin B\}.$$
Here $B\cap(A\setminus B)=\emptyset$.

iv. The complement of a set $A$ is
$$X\setminus A=\{x\mid x\notin A\}.$$
Here $A\cup(X\setminus A)=X$ and $A\cap(X\setminus A)=\emptyset$.

v. The distributive identities are
$$A\cap(B\cup C)=(A\cap B)\cup(A\cap C)$$
and
$$A\cup(B\cap C)=(A\cup B)\cap(A\cup C).$$

vi. The handout lists the following as equivalent:

i. $A\subseteq B$.

ii. $A\cup B=B$.

iii. $A\cap B=A$.

iv. $X\setminus B\subseteq X\setminus A$.

v. $A\cap(X\setminus B)=\emptyset$.

vi. $B\cup(X\setminus A)=X$.

vii. $A\cup B=A\cup(B\setminus A)$.

vii. $A\subseteq A\cup B$ and $B\subseteq A\cup B$.

viii. $A\cap B\subseteq A$ and $A\cap B\subseteq B$.

ix. The Cartesian product is
$$A\times B=\{(x,y)\mid x\in A,\ y\in B\}.$$
