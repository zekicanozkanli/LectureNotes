
> [!thm] [[Theorem 1 (Characterization By Closed Sets)]]: Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be a topological space and $\mathcal T_K$ be the collection of $\mathcal T$-closed subsets of $X$. Then $\mathcal T_K$ possesses the following properties:
> i. $\emptyset, X\in \mathcal T_K$.
> ii. For every $n \geq 1$ and every $K_1, \dots, K_n \in \mathcal T_K$, $$\bigcup_{i=1}^{n}K_i \in \mathcal T_K. \qquad \textit{(closure under finite union)} $$
> iii. For every indexed family $(K_i)_{i \in I} \in \mathcal T_K$, $$\bigcap_{i \in I}K_i \in \mathcal T_K. \qquad \textit{(closure under arbitrary intersections)}$$
> Conversely, if a nonempty collection $\mathcal T_K$ of subsets of $X$ satisfies (i), (ii), and (iii), then $$\mathcal T=\{U\subseteq X : X\setminus U\in\mathcal T_K\}.$$

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Use De Morgan's Law.
