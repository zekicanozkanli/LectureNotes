> [!prop] [[Proposition 3 (Composition Of Continuous Functions)]]: Let $(X,\mathcal T)$, $(Y,\mathcal V)$, and $(Z,\mathcal U)$ be topological spaces. Let $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$ be continuous functions. Then the composite function $g\circ f:X\to Z$ is continuous.


^nt-76532c06edd786c2

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Let $U\in\mathcal U$. Because $g:(Y,\mathcal V)\to(Z,\mathcal U)$ is $\mathcal V$-$\mathcal U$ continuous, $g^{-1}(U)\in\mathcal V$, by [[Definition 5 (Continuous Functions)]].
>
> Since $f:(X,\mathcal T)\to(Y,\mathcal V)$ is $\mathcal T$-$\mathcal V$ continuous,
> $$f^{-1}(g^{-1}(U))=(g\circ f)^{-1}(U)\in\mathcal T.$$
