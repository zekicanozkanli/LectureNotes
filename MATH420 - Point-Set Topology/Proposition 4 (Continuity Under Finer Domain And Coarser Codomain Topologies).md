> [!prop] [[Proposition 4 (Continuity Under Finer Domain And Coarser Codomain Topologies)]]: Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ and $(Y,\mathcal V)$ be two topological spaces and $f:X\to Y$ be a $\mathcal T$-$\mathcal V$ continuous function. Let $(X,\mathcal T_1)$ and $(Y,\mathcal V_1)$ be topological spaces such that $\mathcal T\subseteq\mathcal T_1$ and $\mathcal V_1\subseteq\mathcal V$. Then $f:X\to Y$ is $\mathcal T_1$-$\mathcal V_1$ continuous.


^nt-78d1b945e9e06645

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Let $V\in\mathcal V_1$. Since $\mathcal V_1\subseteq\mathcal V$, $V\in\mathcal V$. As $f:X\to Y$ is $\mathcal T$-$\mathcal V$ continuous, $f^{-1}(V)\in\mathcal T$, by [[Definition 5 (Continuous Functions)]]. Because $\mathcal T\subseteq\mathcal T_1$, $f^{-1}(V)\in\mathcal T_1$.
>
> Therefore $f$ is $\mathcal T_1$-$\mathcal V_1$ continuous.
