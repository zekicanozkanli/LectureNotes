> [!prop] [[Proposition 2 (Bounds For Intersections And Unions Of Topologies)]]: Let $X\ne\emptyset$ and $\{\mathcal T_i\}_{i\in I}$ be a collection of topologies on $X$. Then:
>
> i. $\mathcal T=\bigcap_{i\in I}\mathcal T_i$ is coarser than $\mathcal T_i$ for each $i\in I$.
>
> ii. If $\mathcal T=\bigcup_{i\in I}\mathcal T_i$ is a topology on $X$, then it is finer than $\mathcal T_i$ for each $i\in I$.
