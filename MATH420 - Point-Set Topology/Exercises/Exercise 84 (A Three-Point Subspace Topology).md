
> [!ex] [Exercise 84 (A Three-Point Subspace Topology)](Exercise%2084%20%28A%20Three-Point%20Subspace%20Topology%29.md): Let $X=\{a,b,c,d\}$, $\mathcal{T}=\{\emptyset,\{b\},\{a,b\},\{b,d\},\{a,b,d\},X\}$, and $Y=\{a,c,d\}$. Find $\mathcal{T}_Y$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Taking all intersections $U\cap Y$ with $U\in\mathcal{T}$ gives $\mathcal{T}_Y=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{d\},\{a,d\},Y\}$.$\blacksquare$
