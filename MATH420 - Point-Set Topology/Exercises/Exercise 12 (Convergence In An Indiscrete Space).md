# Exercise 12 (Convergence In An Indiscrete Space)

Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be the indiscrete topological space, and $(x_n)$ be a sequence of points in $X$. Show that $(x_n)$ converges to every point $x$ in $X$.

^nt-fdf49712f02a1e0b

###### Examples.

i. If $X=\mathbb Z^+$, $\mathcal T$ is the trivial topology on $X$, and $(x_n)$ is any sequence of points in $\mathbb Z^+$, then $x_n\to1$, $x_n\to2$, $x_n\to3$, $\ldots$ as $n\to\infty$.

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Let $x\in X$. Since $\mathcal T$ is the indiscrete or trivial topology on $X$, there is only one $\mathcal T$-open set containing $x$. It is the set $X$ itself, by [[Definition 1 (Topological Space)]]. Because $x_n\in X$ for any $n\ge1$, $\lim_{n\to\infty}x_n=x$, by [[Definition 10 (Convergence Of A Sequence)]].
