
> [!ex] [[Exercise 23 (Enlarging a Basis)]]: Let $\mathcal B$ be a basis for a topology $\mathcal T$ on $X$, and let $\mathcal B\prime$ be a class of subsets of $X$ satisfying $\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal B\prime\subseteq\mathcal T$. Show that $\mathcal B\prime$ is also a basis for $\mathcal T$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Let $U\in\mathcal T$ and $x\in U$. Because $\mathcal B$ is a basis for $\mathcal T$, there is $B\in\mathcal B$ with $x\in B\subseteq U$. Since $\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal B\prime$, this $B$ belongs to $\mathcal B\prime$. For every $U\in\mathcal T$ and every $x\in U$ there is therefore $B\in\mathcal B\prime$ such that $x\in B\subseteq U$. By [[Proposition 6 (Local Characterization Of A Basis)]], $\mathcal B\prime$ is a basis for $\mathcal T$.$\blacksquare$
