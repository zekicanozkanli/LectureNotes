
> [!ex] [Exercise 72 (Neighbourhoods in a Five-Point Space)](Exercise%2072%20%28Neighbourhoods%20in%20a%20Five-Point%20Space%29.md): Let $X=\{a,b,c,d,e\}$ and $\mathcal{T}=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{a,b\},\{a,c,d\},\{a,b,c,d\},\{a,b,e\},X\}$. Determine $\eta(c)$ and $\eta(e)$.

###### Solution.
> [!pf] *Solution.* By [Definition 22 (Neighbourhoods and Neighbourhood Systems)](../Definition%2022%20%28Neighbourhoods%20and%20Neighbourhood%20Systems%29.md), $\eta(x)$ consists of the supersets of open sets containing $x$. The open sets containing $c$ are $\{a,c,d\}$, $\{a,b,c,d\}$, and $X$. Hence $\eta(c)=\{\{a,c,d\},\{a,b,c,d\},\{a,c,d,e\},X\}$. The open sets containing $e$ are $\{a,b,e\}$ and $X$, giving $\eta(e)=\{\{a,b,e\},\{a,b,c,e\},\{a,b,d,e\},X\}$.$\blacksquare$
