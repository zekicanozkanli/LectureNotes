
> [!ex] [[Exercise 63 (Subbasic Closure Test Fails)]]: Let $X=\{a,b,c,d\}$ and let $\mathcal T$ be generated by $\mathcal S=\{\{a,b\},\{a,c\},\{d\}\}$. For $A=\{b,c\}$, check whether $a\in\overline A$ and compare the basis and subbase tests.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The generated topology is $\mathcal T=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{d\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\},\{a,d\},\{a,b,d\},\{a,c,d\},\{a,b,c\},X\}$. Its basis of finite subbasic intersections is $\mathcal B=\mathcal S^\cap=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{d\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\},X\}$. Since $a\in\{a\}\in\mathcal B$ and $\{a\}\cap A=\emptyset$, [[Proposition 7 (Closure via a Basis)]] gives $a\notin\overline A$. However, the subbasic sets containing $a$ are $\{a,b\}$ and $\{a,c\}$, and $\{a,b\}\cap A=\{b\}\ne\emptyset$ and $\{a,c\}\cap A=\{c\}\ne\emptyset$. Thus checking only subbasic sets does not establish closure membership.$\blacksquare$
