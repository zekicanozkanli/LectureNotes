
> [!ex] [[Exercise 51 (Zero Sets of Continuous Functions)]]: Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be a topological space and $(\mathbb R,\mathcal U)$ the usual topological space. If $f:X\to\mathbb R$ is continuous, determine whether $A=\{x\in X\mid f(x)=0\}$ is closed.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Note that $\{0\}$ is closed in the usual topology on $\mathbb R$. Since $f$ is continuous, $f^{-1}(\{0\})=\{x\in X\mid f(x)=0\}=A$ is closed in $X$.$\blacksquare$
