
> [!ex] [[Exercise 48 (Closure and Boundary Identities)]]: Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be a topological space and $A,B\subseteq X$. Prove the following identities and inclusions, and determine the requested boundary relations.
> (a) $A$ is closed if and only if $A=\overline A$.
> (b) $\overline A=A^\circ\cup\partial A$.
> (c) $\overline A$ is the smallest closed subset of $X$ containing $A$.
> (d) $A^\circ=X\setminus\overline{X\setminus A}$.
> (e) $\operatorname{ext}(A)=X\setminus\overline A$.
> (f) $\partial A=\overline A\cap\overline{X\setminus A}$.
> (g) $A^\circ\subseteq(\overline A)^\circ$.
> (h) $\operatorname{ext}(A)=\operatorname{ext}(\overline A)$.
> (i) $\partial\overline A\subseteq\partial A$.
> (j) $\overline{\overline A}=\overline A$.
> (k) $\overline{A\cap B}\subseteq\overline A\cap\overline B$.
> (l) $\overline{A\cup B}=\overline A\cup\overline B$.
> (m) $\partial(A^\circ)\subseteq\partial A$.
> (n) How does $\partial(A\cup B)$ relate to $\partial A\cup\partial B$?
> (o) $\partial A=\partial(X\setminus A)$.
> (p) If $A\subseteq B$, then $\overline A\subseteq\overline B$.
> (q) How does $\partial(\operatorname{ext}(A))$ relate to $\partial A$?

> [!rem] Supplied proofs for (a), (c)–(f), (h)–(l), and (p) are consolidated in [[Theorem 12 (Closure Properties)]]; the proof of (b) is in [[Definition 19 (Closure)]] and the proof of (o) is in [[Definition 18 (Boundary)]]. No proof of (g), (m), (n), or (q) is supplied.
