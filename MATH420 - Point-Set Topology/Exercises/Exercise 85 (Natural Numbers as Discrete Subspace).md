
> [!ex] [Exercise 85 (Natural Numbers as Discrete Subspace)](Exercise%2085%20%28Natural%20Numbers%20as%20Discrete%20Subspace%29.md): Let $\mathbb{R}$ have its usual topology and let $Y=\mathbb{N}$. Find the induced topology on $Y$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* For every $n\in\mathbb{N}$, the interval $(n-1,n+1)$ is open in $\mathbb{R}$ and $(n-1,n+1)\cap\mathbb{N}=\{n\}$. Thus every singleton is open in the subspace, and the induced topology on $\mathbb{N}$ is discrete.$\blacksquare$
