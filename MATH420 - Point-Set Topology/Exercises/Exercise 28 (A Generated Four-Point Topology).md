
> [!ex] [[Exercise 28 (A Generated Four-Point Topology)]]: Let $X=\{1,2,3,4\}$ and $\mathcal S=\{\{1\},\{3\},\{2,4\},\{1,3,4\}\}$. Find the topology $\mathcal T$ on $X$ generated by $\mathcal S$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The finite intersections give the basis $$\mathcal B=\mathcal S^{\cap}=\{\emptyset,\{1\},\{3\},\{2,4\},\{4\},\{1,3,4\},X\}.$$ Taking unions of basis elements gives $$\mathcal T=\{\emptyset,\{1\},\{1,3\},\{1,2,4\},\{1,4\},\{1,3,4\},X,\{3\},\{2,3,4\},\{3,4\},\{2,4\},\{4\}\}.$$$\blacksquare$
