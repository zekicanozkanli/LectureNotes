
> [!ex] [[Exercise 33 (Topologies on Three Points)]]: Let $X=\{a,b,c\}$. Write all possible topologies on $X$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* $\{p,q,r\}=X$.
>
> | Family | Number of distinct topologies |
> | --- | ---: |
> | $\{\emptyset,X\}$ | $1$ |
> | $\{\emptyset,X,\{p\}\}$ | $3$ |
> | $\{\emptyset,X,\{p,q\}\}$ | $3$ |
> | $\{\emptyset,X,\{p\},\{p,q\}\}$ | $6$ |
> | $\{\emptyset,X,\{p\},\{q,r\}\}$ | $3$ |
> | $\{\emptyset,X,\{p\},\{q\},\{p,q\}\}$ | $3$ |
> | $\{\emptyset,X,\{p\},\{p,q\},\{p,r\}\}$ | $3$ |
> | $\{\emptyset,X,\{p\},\{q\},\{p,q\},\{p,r\}\}$ | $6$ |
> | $\{\emptyset,X,\{p\},\{q\},\{r\},\{p,q\},\{p,r\},\{q,r\}\}$ | $1$ |
>
> $X=\{a,b,c\}$.
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_{1}&=\{\emptyset,X\}
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_{2}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{3}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{4}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{c\}\}
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_{5}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a,b\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{6}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{7}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b,c\}\}
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_{8}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{a,b\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{9}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{a,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{10}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b\},\{a,b\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{11}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b\},\{b,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{12}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{c\},\{a,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{13}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{c\},\{b,c\}\}
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_{14}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{b,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{15}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b\},\{a,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{16}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{c\},\{a,b\}\}
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_{17}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{b\},\{a,b\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{18}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{c\},\{a,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{19}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b\},\{c\},\{b,c\}\}
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_{20}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{21}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b\},\{a,b\},\{b,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{22}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{c\},\{a,c\},\{b,c\}\}
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_{23}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{b\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{24}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{c\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{25}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{b\},\{a,b\},\{b,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{26}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b\},\{c\},\{a,b\},\{b,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{27}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{c\},\{a,c\},\{b,c\}\}\\
> \mathcal T_{28}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b\},\{c\},\{a,c\},\{b,c\}\}
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_{29}&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{b\},\{c\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\},\{b,c\}\} \blacksquare
> \end{aligned}$$
