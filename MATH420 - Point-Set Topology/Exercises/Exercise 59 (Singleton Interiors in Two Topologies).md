
> [!ex] [[Exercise 59 (Singleton Interiors in Two Topologies)]]: Let $\mathcal T_1$ and $\mathcal T_2$ be the usual and discrete topologies on $\mathbb R$, respectively. For $A=\{x\}$ determine its interior in each topology.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* (Case I (usual topology)) No open interval $I$ satisfies $x\in I\subseteq\{x\}$, so $A^\circ=\emptyset$. (Case II (discrete topology)) Every subset is open, so $\{x\}=A\in\mathcal T_2$ and $x\in A\subseteq A$. Thus $x\in A^\circ$, and $A^\circ=A$.$\blacksquare$
