
> [!ex] [[Exercise 55 (Integers in Particular Point Topology)]]: Let $X$ be a set, fix $x_0\in X$, and set $\mathcal T=\{U\subseteq X\mid x_0\in U\}\cup\{\emptyset\}$. (a) Show that $\mathcal T$ is a topology, called the included point or particular point topology. (b) For $X=\mathbb Q$, $x_0=1$, and $A=\mathbb Z$, determine $A^\circ$, $\operatorname{ext}(A)$, $\overline A$, the isolated-point set $I(A)$, and the derived set $\widetilde A$. (c) Determine whether $A$ is dense in $\mathbb Q$.

> [!rem] The definitions of $I(A)$ and $\widetilde A$ are {unresolved} in the supplied material. The topology construction also occurs in [[Exercise 56 (Particular Point Topology Computations)]].
