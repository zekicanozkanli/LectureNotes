
> [!ex] [Exercise 91 (Interior and Closure of Segment)](Exercise%2091%20%28Interior%20and%20Closure%20of%20Segment%29.md): In the usual plane, let $A=(1,2]\times\{1\}$. Find $A^\circ$ and $\overline{A}$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* By [Theorem 34 (Interior and Closure of Products)](../Theorem%2034%20%28Interior%20and%20Closure%20of%20Products%29.md), $A^\circ=(1,2]^\circ\times\{1\}^\circ=(1,2)\times\emptyset=\emptyset$. Also, $\overline{A}=\overline{(1,2]}\times\overline{\{1\}}=[1,2]\times\{1\}=\{(x,1)\mid1\le x\le2\}$.$\blacksquare$
