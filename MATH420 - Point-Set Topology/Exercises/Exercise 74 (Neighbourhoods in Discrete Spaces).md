
> [!ex] [Exercise 74 (Neighbourhoods in Discrete Spaces)](Exercise%2074%20%28Neighbourhoods%20in%20Discrete%20Spaces%29.md): Let $(X,\mathcal{T})$ be discrete and $x\in X$. Determine $\eta(x)$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The singleton $\{x\}$ is open and contains $x$. Therefore every $N$ with $x\in N\subseteq X$ is a neighbourhood, and every neighbourhood contains $x$. Hence $\eta(x)=\{N\subseteq X\mid x\in N\}$.$\blacksquare$
