
> [!ex] [Exercise 102 (Cofinite Spaces Are Not Hausdorff)](Exercise%20102%20%28Cofinite%20Spaces%20Are%20Not%20Hausdorff%29.md): Let $X$ be infinite and give it the cofinite topology $\mathcal{T}$. Show that $(X,\mathcal{T})$ is $T_1$ but not Hausdorff.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Let $x,y$ be distinct points of $X$. There are cofinite open sets $U=X\setminus\{x_1,\ldots,x_n\}$ and $V=X\setminus\{y_1,\ldots,y_m\}$ such that $x\in U$, $y\notin U$, $x\notin V$, and $y\in V$. Thus the space is $T_1$. Because $X$ is infinite, such nonempty cofinite open sets always have $U\cap V\ne\emptyset$. Therefore distinct points cannot be separated by disjoint open sets, so $(X,\mathcal{T})$ is not Hausdorff.$\blacksquare$
