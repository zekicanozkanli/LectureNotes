
> [!ex] [[Exercise 29 (A Subbase Generating Discreteness)]]: Let $X=\{a,b,c,d\}$ and $\mathcal S=\{\{a,b\},\{a,c\},\{a,d\},\{b,c\},\{c,d\}\}$. Find and name the topology $\mathcal T$ generated by $\mathcal S$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The finite intersections give $$\mathcal S^{\cap}=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{b\},\{c\},\{d\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\},\{a,d\},\{b,c\},\{c,d\},X\},$$ which is a basis for $\mathcal T$. Its unions are $$\mathcal T=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{b\},\{c\},\{d\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\},\{a,d\},\{b,c\},\{b,d\},\{c,d\},\{a,b,c\},\{a,c,d\},\{a,b,d\},\{b,c,d\},X\}.$$ Thus $\mathcal T$ is the discrete topology on $X$.$\blacksquare$
