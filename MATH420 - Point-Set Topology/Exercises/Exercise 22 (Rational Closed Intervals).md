
> [!ex] [[Exercise 22 (Rational Closed Intervals)]]: Let $X=\mathbb R$ and $\mathcal B=\{[p,q]\mid p,q\in\mathbb Q,\ p<q\}$. Determine whether $\mathcal B$ is a basis for any topology on $\mathbb R$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Let $B_1=[1,2]$ and $B_2=[2,3]$. Then $2\in B_1\cap B_2=\{2\}$. There are no rational numbers $p<q$ such that $2\in[p,q]\subseteq\{2\}$. The second condition in [[Theorem 4 (Conditions For A Collection To Form A Basis)]] fails, so $\mathcal B$ cannot be a basis for any topology on $\mathbb R$.$\blacksquare$
