
> [!ex] [Exercise 89 (Product of Two Two-Point Spaces)](Exercise%2089%20%28Product%20of%20Two%20Two-Point%20Spaces%29.md): Let $X=\{a,b\}$, $\mathcal{T}=\{\emptyset,\{a\},X\}$, $Y=\{1,2\}$, and $\mathcal{V}=\{\emptyset,\{2\},Y\}$. Find the product topology on $X\times Y$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The product set is $\{(a,1),(a,2),(b,1),(b,2)\}$. The rectangular basis consists of $\emptyset$, $\{(a,2)\}$, $\{(a,1),(a,2)\}$, $\{(a,2),(b,2)\}$, and $X\times Y$. Taking arbitrary unions adds the set $\{(a,1),(a,2),(b,2)\}$, giving six open sets in total. This three-point open set is not a rectangle.$\blacksquare$
