
> [!ex] [Exercise 86 (Closed Sets of a Subspace)](Exercise%2086%20%28Closed%20Sets%20of%20a%20Subspace%29.md): Let $X=\{a,b,c,d\}$, $\mathcal{T}=\{\emptyset,\{b\},\{a,b\},\{b,d\},\{a,b,d\},X\}$, and $Y=\{a,b,c\}$. Find all closed subsets of the subspace $Y$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The induced open sets are $\mathcal{T}_Y=\{\emptyset,\{b\},\{a,b\},Y\}$. Taking complements in $Y$ gives $(\mathcal{T}_Y)_K=\{\emptyset,\{c\},\{a,c\},Y\}$. Alternatively, the ambient closed sets are $\mathcal{T}_K=\{\emptyset,\{c\},\{a,c\},\{c,d\},\{a,c,d\},X\}$. Intersecting each with $Y$ gives the same family. In this example $(\mathcal{T}_Y)_K\nsubseteq\mathcal{T}_K$.$\blacksquare$
