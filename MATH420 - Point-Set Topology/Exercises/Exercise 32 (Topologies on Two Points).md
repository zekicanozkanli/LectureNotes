
> [!ex] [[Exercise 32 (Topologies on Two Points)]]: Let $X=\{a,b\}$. Write all possible topologies on $X$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Since $X=\{a,b\}$, its power set is $\mathcal P(X)=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{b\},X\}$. And the indiscrete topology contains only $\emptyset$ and $X$. Other topologies must be in between.
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \mathcal T_0&=\{\emptyset,X\},\\
> \mathcal T_1&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\}\},\\
> \mathcal T_2&=\{\emptyset,X,\{b\}\},\\
> \mathcal T_3&=\{\emptyset,X,\{a\},\{b\}\}.
> \end{aligned}$$
