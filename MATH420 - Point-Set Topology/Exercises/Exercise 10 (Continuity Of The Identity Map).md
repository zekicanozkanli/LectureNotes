# Exercise 10 (Continuity Of The Identity Map)

Let $(X,\mathcal T_1)$ and $(X,\mathcal T_2)$ be topological spaces. Consider the function $i:X\to X$, $x\mapsto i(x)=x$. Then
$$i\text{ is }\mathcal T_1\text{-}\mathcal T_2\text{ continuous}\iff\mathcal T_2\subseteq\mathcal T_1.$$
