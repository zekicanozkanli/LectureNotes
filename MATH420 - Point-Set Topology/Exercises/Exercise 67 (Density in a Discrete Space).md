
> [!ex] [[Exercise 67 (Density in a Discrete Space)]]: Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be a discrete topological space. Determine all its dense subsets.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Every subset $A\subseteq X$ is closed and open, so $\overline A=A$. Therefore $A$ is dense if and only if $\overline A=X$, equivalently $A=X$.$\blacksquare$
