
> [!ex] [[Exercise 30 (Continuity from Three Subbasic Preimages)]]: Let $X=\{a,b,c,d\}$ and $\mathcal S=\{\{a,b\},\{a,c\},\{d\}\}$. The generated topology is $$\mathcal T=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{d\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\},\{a,d\},\{a,b,d\},\{a,c,d\},\{a,b,c\},X\}.$$ Let $f:X\to X$ be defined by $f(a)=f(b)=f(c)=c$ and $f(d)=a$. Show that $f$ is continuous.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* The subbasic inverse images are $$f^{-1}(\{a,b\})=\{d\}\in\mathcal T,\qquad f^{-1}(\{a,c\})=X\in\mathcal T,\qquad f^{-1}(\{d\})=\emptyset\in\mathcal T.$$ Thus $f^{-1}(S)\in\mathcal T$ for every $S\in\mathcal S$, and [[Theorem 7 (Continuity via a Subbase)]] implies that $f$ is continuous.$\blacksquare$
