# Exercise 8 (Continuity Of Constant Functions)

Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ and $(Y,\mathcal V)$ be two topological spaces. If $f:X\to Y$ is a constant function, show that $f$ is $\mathcal T$-$\mathcal V$ continuous.

^nt-a3687c51595dc57e

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Since $f$ is a constant function, there exists $y_0\in Y$ such that $f(x)=y_0$ for all $x\in X$. Let $V\in\mathcal V$.
> i. If $y_0\in V$, then $f^{-1}(V)=X\in\mathcal T$.
> ii. Assume that $y_0\notin V$. Then $f^{-1}(V)=\emptyset\in\mathcal T$.
>
> Therefore $f$ is $\mathcal T$-$\mathcal V$ continuous, by [[Definition 5 (Continuous Functions)]].
