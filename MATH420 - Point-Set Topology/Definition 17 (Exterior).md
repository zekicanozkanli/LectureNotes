
> [!def] [[Definition 17 (Exterior)]]: Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be a topological space and $A\subseteq X$. An **exterior point** of $A$ is an interior point of $X\setminus A$. The set of all exterior points is the **exterior** of $A$, denoted $\operatorname{ext}(A)=(X\setminus A)^\circ$.

> [!rem] The set $\operatorname{ext}(A)$ is open. Also $A\cap\operatorname{ext}(A)=\emptyset$ and $A^\circ\cap\operatorname{ext}(A)=\emptyset$: indeed, $(X\setminus A)^\circ\subseteq X\setminus A$ and $A\cap(X\setminus A)=\emptyset$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: In the five-point boundary example, $\operatorname{ext}(A)=\{a\}$ (see [[Exercise 62 (Boundary in a Five-Point Space)]]).

> [!eg] Ex. 2: In the four-point closure example, $\operatorname{ext}(A)=\operatorname{ext}(\overline A)=\{d\}$ (see [[Exercise 64 (Closure in a Four-Point Space)]]).
