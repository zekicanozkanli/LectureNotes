> [!prop] [[Proposition 1 (De Morgan's Laws)]]: For subsets $A,B$ of $X$ and a collection $\{A_i\}_{i\in I}$ of subsets of $X$:
>
> i. $X\setminus(A\cup B)=(X\setminus A)\cap(X\setminus B)$.
>
> ii. $X\setminus(A\cap B)=(X\setminus A)\cup(X\setminus B)$.
>
> iii. $X\setminus\left(\bigcup_{i\in I}A_i\right)=\bigcap_{i\in I}(X\setminus A_i)$.
>
> iv. $X\setminus\left(\bigcap_{i\in I}A_i\right)=\bigcup_{i\in I}(X\setminus A_i)$.
