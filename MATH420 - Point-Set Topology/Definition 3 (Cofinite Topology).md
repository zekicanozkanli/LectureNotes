> [!def] [[Definition 3 (Cofinite Topology)]]: Let $X\ne\emptyset$ and
> $$\mathcal T=\{\emptyset\}\cup\{A\mid A\subseteq X,\ A'\text{ is finite}\}.$$
> Then $\mathcal T$ is a topology on $X$. It is called the cofinite topology or the finite complement topology on $X$.
>
> We already know that $\emptyset,X\in\mathcal T_K$. Let $\emptyset\ne A\subsetneq X$. By [[Definition 2 (Closed Sets)]],
> $$A\text{ is }\mathcal T\text{-closed}\iff A'\in\mathcal T.$$
> Also $A'\in\mathcal T\iff A=(A')'$ is finite. In other words, if $\emptyset\ne A\subsetneq X$, then $A$ is $\mathcal T$-closed iff $A$ is finite.
>
> The verification that $\mathcal T$ is a topology is given in [[Exercises/Exercise 2 (Cofinite Topology Axioms)]].
