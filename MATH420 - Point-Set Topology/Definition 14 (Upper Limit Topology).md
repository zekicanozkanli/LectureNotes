> [!def] [[Definition 14 (Upper Limit Topology)]]: Let $X=\mathbb R$ and
> $$\mathcal B=\{(a,b]\mid a,b\in\mathbb R,\ a<b\}.$$
> Then $\mathcal B$ is a base for some topology $\mathcal T$ on $\mathbb R$. $\mathcal T$ is called the upper limit topology on $\mathbb R$.
>
> The verification is in [[Exercises/Exercise 20 (Right-Closed Intervals Form A Basis)]].
