
> [!def] [Definition 29 (Initial Topology)](Definition%2029%20%28Initial%20Topology%29.md): Let $X$ be a nonempty set, let $(Y_i,\mathcal{T}_i)$ be topological spaces, and let $f_i:X\to Y_i$ be functions for $i\in I$. The coarsest (smallest, or weakest) topology on $X$ making every $f_i$ continuous is called the **initial topology** for this family.
