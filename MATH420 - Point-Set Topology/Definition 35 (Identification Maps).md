
> [!def] [Definition 35 (Identification Maps)](Definition%2035%20%28Identification%20Maps%29.md): A function $f:(X,\mathcal{T})\to(Y,\mathcal{V})$ is an **identification map** if:  
> i. $f$ is onto.  
> ii. $\mathcal{V}$ is the finest topology on $Y$ for which $f$ is continuous.

###### Motivation.
Any onto function $f:X\to Y$ determines the partition $\{f^{-1}(\{y\})\mid y\in Y\}$ of $X$. Let $Y^*$ be the associated identification space and let $\pi:X\to Y^*$ send each point to its fibre. The terminology reflects the relation between $Y$ and this space.
