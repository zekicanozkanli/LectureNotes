
> [!def] [Definition 37 (Collapsing a Subspace)](Definition%2037%20%28Collapsing%20a%20Subspace%29.md): Let $Y$ be a subspace of a topological space $(X,\mathcal{T})$. Define $x\sim_Y y$ if and only if $x=y$ or $x,y\in Y$. The quotient space $X/{\sim_Y}$, with its quotient topology $\mathcal{V}$, is denoted $X/Y$ and is obtained by **collapsing $Y$ to a point**. Its equivalence classes are $Y$ when $Y\ne\emptyset$, and the singletons $\{x\}$ for $x\in X\setminus Y$.
