> [!prop] [[Proposition 5 (Homeomorphism Is An Equivalence Relation)]]: The relation in any collection of topological spaces defined by “$X$ is homeomorphic to $Y$” is an equivalence relation.
>
> By the [[Fundamental Theorem On Equivalence Relations]], any collection of topological spaces can be partitioned into classes of topologically equivalent spaces.
