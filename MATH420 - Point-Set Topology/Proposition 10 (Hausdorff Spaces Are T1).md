
> [!prop] [Proposition 10 (Hausdorff Spaces Are T1)](Proposition%2010%20%28Hausdorff%20Spaces%20Are%20T1%29.md): Every Hausdorff space is a $T_1$-space. The converse need not hold.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The cofinite topology on an infinite set is $T_1$ but not Hausdorff (see [Exercise 102 (Cofinite Spaces Are Not Hausdorff)](Exercises/Exercise%20102%20%28Cofinite%20Spaces%20Are%20Not%20Hausdorff%29.md)).
