
> [!thm] [Theorem 47 (Closed Relations and Hausdorff Quotients)](Theorem%2047%20%28Closed%20Relations%20and%20Hausdorff%20Quotients%29.md): Let $(X,\mathcal{T})$ be a topological space, let $E$ be an equivalence relation on $X$, and let $\pi:X\to X/E$, $\pi(x)=[x]$, be the natural function. If $\pi$ is open and $E\subseteq X\times X$ is closed in the product topology, then $X/E$ with the quotient topology is Hausdorff.
