
> [!def] [Definition 32 (Partitions)](Definition%2032%20%28Partitions%29.md): A collection $\mathcal{P}=\{A_i\mid i\in I\}$ of nonempty subsets of $X$ is a **partition** of $X$ if:  
> i. $A_i\cap A_j=\emptyset$ whenever $i\ne j$.  
> ii. $\bigcup_{i\in I}A_i=X$.
