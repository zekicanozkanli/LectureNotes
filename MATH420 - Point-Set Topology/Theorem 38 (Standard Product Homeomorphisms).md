
> [!thm] [Theorem 38 (Standard Product Homeomorphisms)](Theorem%2038%20%28Standard%20Product%20Homeomorphisms%29.md): For topological spaces $X,Y$ with product topologies:  
> i. $X\times Y$ is homeomorphic to $Y\times X$.  
> ii. For $x_0\in X$, the slice $\{x_0\}\times Y$ is homeomorphic to $Y$.  
> iii. For $y_0\in Y$, the slice $X\times\{y_0\}$ is homeomorphic to $X$.

> [!rem] The source supplies only the homeomorphism formulas; the bijectivity and continuity verifications are not supplied.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* For (i), use $f:X\times Y\to Y\times X$, $f(x,y)=(y,x)$. For (ii), use $f:\{x_0\}\times Y\to Y$, $f(x_0,y)=y$. For (iii), use $f:X\times\{y_0\}\to X$, $f(x,y_0)=x$.$\blacksquare$
