
> [!thm] [Theorem 45 (Hereditary Separation Axioms)](Theorem%2045%20%28Hereditary%20Separation%20Axioms%29.md): If $X$ is a $T_2$-space and $Y\subseteq X$, then the subspace $Y$ is $T_2$. The same conclusion holds for $T_0$ and $T_1$; these are hereditary properties.

> [!rem] Only the $T_2$ case is proved in the supplied page.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Let $x,y\in Y$ with $x\ne y$. Since $X$ is Hausdorff, there are disjoint $U,V\in\mathcal{T}$ with $x\in U$ and $y\in V$. Then $U\cap Y$ and $V\cap Y$ are open in the subspace and contain $x$ and $y$, respectively. Moreover, $(U\cap Y)\cap(V\cap Y)=(U\cap V)\cap Y=\emptyset$. Hence $Y$ is Hausdorff.$\blacksquare$
