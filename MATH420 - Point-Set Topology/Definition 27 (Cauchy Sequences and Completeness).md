
> [!def] [Definition 27 (Cauchy Sequences and Completeness)](Definition%2027%20%28Cauchy%20Sequences%20and%20Completeness%29.md): Let $(X,d)$ be a metric space. A sequence $(x_n)$ is a **Cauchy sequence** if for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $N\in\mathbb{Z}^{+}$ such that $d(x_n,x_m)<\varepsilon$ whenever $n,m\geq N$. The metric space is **complete** if every Cauchy sequence in it converges to a point of $X$.
