
> [!def] [[Definition 4 (Coarser And Finer Topologies)]]: Let $\mathcal T_1$ and $\mathcal T_2$ be two topologies on a nonempty set $X$. Assume that $\mathcal T_1\subseteq\mathcal T_2$. In this case we say that $\mathcal T_1$ is coarser, or smaller, or weaker than $\mathcal T_2$; or that $\mathcal T_2$ is finer, or larger than $\mathcal T_1$.

Observe that the collection $\{\mathcal T_i\}_{i\in I}$ of all topologies on $X$ is partially ordered by class inclusion. We say that two topologies on $X$ are not comparable if neither is coarser than the other.
###### Examples.
i. The indiscrete and discrete topologies on $X$ are the coarsest and finest topologies on $X$, respectively.
