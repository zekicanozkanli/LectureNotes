> [!def] [[Definition 2 (Closed Sets)]]: Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be a topological space. A subset $K$ of $X$ is called $\mathcal T$-closed, or simply closed, if its complement $K'=K^c=X\setminus K$ in $X$ is a $\mathcal T$-open set. In other words,
> $$K\text{ is }\mathcal T\text{-closed}\iff X\setminus K\in\mathcal T.$$
