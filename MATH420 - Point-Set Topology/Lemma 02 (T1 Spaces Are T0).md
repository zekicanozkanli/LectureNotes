
> [!lem] [Lemma 02 (T1 Spaces Are T0)](Lemma%2002%20%28T1%20Spaces%20Are%20T0%29.md): Every $T_1$-space is a $T_0$-space. The converse need not hold.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: On $X=\{a,b,c\}$, $\mathcal{V}=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{a,b\},X\}$ is $T_0$ and is not $T_1$ (see [Exercise 100 (Separation in Three-Point Spaces)](Exercises/Exercise%20100%20%28Separation%20in%20Three-Point%20Spaces%29.md)).
