> [!def] [[Definition 13 (Usual Topology On The Real Line)]]: Let $X=\mathbb R$. Consider the class $\mathcal B$ of all open intervals,
> $$\mathcal B=\{(a,b)\mid a,b\in\mathbb R,\ a<b\}.$$
> Then $\mathcal B$ forms a base for some topology $\mathcal T$ on $\mathbb R$. This topology is called the usual or the standard topology on $\mathbb R$. All open intervals are open with this topology.
>
> The verification is in [[Exercises/Exercise 18 (Open Intervals Form A Basis On The Real Line)]].
