
> [!thm] [Theorem 29 (Open Subspaces and Ambient Openness)](Theorem%2029%20%28Open%20Subspaces%20and%20Ambient%20Openness%29.md): For a subspace $Y$ of $(X,\mathcal{T})$, $\mathcal{T}_Y\subseteq\mathcal{T}$ if and only if $Y\in\mathcal{T}$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* ($\Rightarrow$) Since $Y\in\mathcal{T}_Y$, the inclusion implies $Y\in\mathcal{T}$. ($\Leftarrow$) If $Y$ is open in $X$, then $U\cap Y\in\mathcal{T}$ for every $U\in\mathcal{T}$. Hence every member of $\mathcal{T}_Y$ belongs to $\mathcal{T}$.$\blacksquare$
