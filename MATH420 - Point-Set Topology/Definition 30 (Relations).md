
> [!def] [Definition 30 (Relations)](Definition%2030%20%28Relations%29.md): A **relation from $X$ to $Y$** is a subset of $X\times Y$. A **relation on $X$** is a relation from $X$ to itself, that is, a subset of $X\times X$. For $R\subseteq X\times X$, write $xRy$ when $(x,y)\in R$; specifying the meaning of $xRy$ specifies the relation.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: For a positive integer $m$, congruence modulo $m$ is the relation $x\sim y\iff x\equiv y\pmod m$ on $\mathbb{Z}$, equivalently $R=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}\mid x\equiv y\pmod m\}$.
