
> [!ex] [[Exercise 2 (Cube Root Computation)]]: Find the cube roots of $1+i$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Write $1+i=\sqrt2\,e^{i\pi/4}$. By [[Theorem 3 (Roots in Polar Form)]], the roots are $\displaystyle (\sqrt2)^{1/3}e^{i\pi/12}$, $\displaystyle (\sqrt2)^{1/3}e^{i(\pi/12+2\pi/3)}$, and $\displaystyle (\sqrt2)^{1/3}e^{i(\pi/12+4\pi/3)}$. The diagram places them on the circle of radius $2^{1/6}$ with successive angular gaps $2\pi/3$. $\blacksquare$
