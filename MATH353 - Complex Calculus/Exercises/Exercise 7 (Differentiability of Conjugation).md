
> [!ex] [[Exercise 7 (Differentiability of Conjugation)]]: Show that $f(z)=\overline z$ is nowhere complex differentiable.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* For every $z$, $\displaystyle \frac{f(z+\Delta z)-f(z)}{\Delta z}=\frac{\overline{z+\Delta z}-\overline z}{\Delta z}=\frac{\overline{\Delta z}}{\Delta z}$. Write $\Delta z=\Delta x+i\Delta y$. Along $\Delta y=0$ the quotient is $\Delta x/\Delta x=1$; along $\Delta x=0$ it is $(-i\Delta y)/(i\Delta y)=-1$. Thus the limit does not exist at any $z$. $\blacksquare$
