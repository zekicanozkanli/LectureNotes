
> [!ex] [[Exercise 5 (Rational Limit at Infinity)]]: Find $\lim_{z\to\infty}(2z+3)/(z+i)$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* By [[Theorem 7 (Reciprocal Limit Criteria)]], $\displaystyle \lim_{z\to\infty}\frac{2z+3}{z+i}=\lim_{z\to0}\frac{2/z+3}{1/z+i}=\lim_{z\to0}\frac{2+3z}{1+iz}=\frac21=2$. $\blacksquare$
