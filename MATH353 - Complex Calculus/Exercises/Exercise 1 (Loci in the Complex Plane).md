
> [!ex] [[Exercise 1 (Loci in the Complex Plane)]]: Represent the sets $\{z:\operatorname{Re}z<1\}$, $\{z:|z|=1\}$, $\{z:|z-2i|<1\}$, and $\{z:|z-1|+|z-2i|=5\}$ in the complex plane.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The first set is the half-plane to the left of the excluded line $x=1$. The second is the unit circle centered at the origin. The third is the open disk of radius $1$ centered at $2i$, with its boundary excluded. The fourth is the ellipse with foci $1$ and $2i$ whose sum of distances from the foci is $5$, as drawn in the source. $\blacksquare$
