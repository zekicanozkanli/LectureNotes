
> [!ex] [[Exercise 3 (Limit of the Square)]]: Show that $\lim_{z\to i}z^2=-1$ using the $\varepsilon$–$\delta$ definition.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* For $\varepsilon>0$, choose $0<\delta<\min\{\varepsilon/3,1\}$. When $0<|z-i|<\delta$, the diagram bounds the distance from $z$ to $-i$ by $|z+i|<3$. Therefore $|z^2-(-1)|=|z^2+1|=|z-i|\,|z+i|<3\delta<\varepsilon$, as required. $\blacksquare$
