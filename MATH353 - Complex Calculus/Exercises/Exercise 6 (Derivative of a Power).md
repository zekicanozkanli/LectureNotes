
> [!ex] [[Exercise 6 (Derivative of a Power)]]: Compute the derivative of $f(z)=z^n$, where $n\in\mathbb Z^+$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Expand the difference quotient: $\displaystyle f'(z)=\lim_{\Delta z\to0}\frac{(z+\Delta z)^n-z^n}{\Delta z}=\lim_{\Delta z\to0}\frac{\Delta z\bigl(nz^{n-1}+\binom n2z^{n-2}\Delta z+\cdots+(\Delta z)^{n-1}\bigr)}{\Delta z}=nz^{n-1}$. $\blacksquare$
