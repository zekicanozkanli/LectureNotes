
> [!ex] [[Exercise 4 (Conjugate Quotient Limit)]]: Show that $\lim_{z\to0}\overline z/z$ does not exist.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Along the real axis $z=x$, $\overline z/z=x/x=1$ and the limit is $1$. Along the imaginary axis $z=iy$, $\overline z/z=(-iy)/(iy)=-1$ and the limit is $-1$. These different limits rule out a complex limit. $\blacksquare$
