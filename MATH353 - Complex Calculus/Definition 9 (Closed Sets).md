
> [!def] [[Definition 9 (Closed Sets)]]: A set $S\subseteq\mathbb C$ is **closed** when
> i. Its complement $S^c=\mathbb C\setminus S$ is open.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The pictured region with its whole boundary included is closed. The pictured square with some boundary edges included and another omitted is neither closed nor open.
