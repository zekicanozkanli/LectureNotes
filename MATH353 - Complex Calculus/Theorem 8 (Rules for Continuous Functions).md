
> [!thm] [[Theorem 8 (Rules for Continuous Functions)]]: If $f$ and $g$ are continuous at $z_0$, then $f+g$ and $fg$ are continuous there; $f/g$ is continuous there when $g(z_0)\ne0$. Compositions of continuous functions are continuous wherever defined. If $f$ is continuous at $z_0$ and $f(z_0)\ne0$, then $f$ is nonzero in a neighborhood of $z_0$. If $u$ and $v$ are continuous at $(x_0,y_0)$, then $f(z)=u(x,y)+iv(x,y)$ is continuous at $z_0=x_0+iy_0$.
