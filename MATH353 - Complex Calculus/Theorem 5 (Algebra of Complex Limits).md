
> [!thm] [[Theorem 5 (Algebra of Complex Limits)]]: If $\lim_{z\to z_0}f(z)=w_0$ and $\lim_{z\to z_0}g(z)=w_1$, then $\lim_{z\to z_0}(f(z)+g(z))=w_0+w_1$ and $\lim_{z\to z_0}f(z)g(z)=w_0w_1$. If $w_1\ne0$, then $\lim_{z\to z_0}f(z)/g(z)=w_0/w_1$.
