
> [!prop] [[Proposition 3 (Polar Products and Powers)]]: If $z_1=r_1e^{i\theta_1}$ and $z_2=r_2e^{i\theta_2}$, then $z_1z_2=r_1r_2e^{i(\theta_1+\theta_2)}$, so $|z_1z_2|=r_1r_2$ and $\arg(z_1z_2)=\arg z_1+\arg z_2+2n\pi$, $n\in\mathbb Z$. For a positive integer $n$, $z^n=r^ne^{in\theta}$ and $|z^n|=r^n$.

> [!rem] The product diagram adds the two arguments and multiplies the radii.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: $e^{i2\theta}=(e^{i\theta})^2=\cos(2\theta)+i\sin(2\theta)=(\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta)+i(2\cos\theta\sin\theta)$.
