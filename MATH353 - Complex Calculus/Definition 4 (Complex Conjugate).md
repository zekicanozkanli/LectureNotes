
> [!def] [[Definition 4 (Complex Conjugate)]]: The **complex conjugate** of $z=x+iy$ is $\overline z=x-iy$.

> [!rem] Conjugation reflects a point across the real axis, as shown by the pair $x+iy$ and $x-iy$ in the diagram.
