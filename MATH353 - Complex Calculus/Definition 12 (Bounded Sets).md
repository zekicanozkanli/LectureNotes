
> [!def] [[Definition 12 (Bounded Sets)]]: A set $S\subseteq\mathbb C$ is **bounded** if it is contained in a disk:
> i. There exist $z_0\in\mathbb C$ and $R>0$ such that $S\subseteq U_R(z_0)=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z-z_0|<R\}$.
