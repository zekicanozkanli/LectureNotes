
> [!prop] [[Proposition 6 (Stereographic Images of Circles)]]: Let $C$ be a circle on the unit sphere $S$. Its stereographic image $T=\pi(C)$ in $\mathbb C^*$ is a circle or a line.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Write $C=\{AX+BY+CZ=D\}\cap\{X^2+Y^2+Z^2=1\}$. Substituting the inverse coordinates from [[Definition 16 (Riemann Sphere)]] gives $\displaystyle A\frac{2x}{1+x^2+y^2}+B\frac{2y}{1+x^2+y^2}+C\frac{x^2+y^2-1}{1+x^2+y^2}=D$. Multiplication by $1+x^2+y^2$ yields $2Ax+2By+C(x^2+y^2)-C=D(x^2+y^2)+D$, or $2Ax+2By+(C-D)(x^2+y^2)=D+C$. (Case I ($C-D=0$)) This is a line. (Case II ($C-D\ne0$)) This is a circle. $\blacksquare$
