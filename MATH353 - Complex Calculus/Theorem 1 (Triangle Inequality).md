
> [!thm] [[Theorem 1 (Triangle Inequality)]]: For $z,w\in\mathbb C$, $\bigl||z|-|w|\bigr|\leq |z+w|\leq |z|+|w|$. More generally, $|z_1+\cdots+z_n|\leq |z_1|+\cdots+|z_n|$.
