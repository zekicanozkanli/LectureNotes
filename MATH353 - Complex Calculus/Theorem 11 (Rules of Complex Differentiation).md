
> [!thm] [[Theorem 11 (Rules of Complex Differentiation)]]: For differentiable functions, $(f+g)'=f'+g'$, $(fg)'=f'g+fg'$, $\displaystyle (f/g)'=\frac{f'g-fg'}{g^2}$ wherever $g\ne0$, and $(f\circ g)'(z)=f'(g(z))g'(z)$.
