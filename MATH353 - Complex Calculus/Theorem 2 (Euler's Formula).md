
> [!thm] [[Theorem 2 (Euler's Formula)]]: For real $\theta$, $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* The supplied power-series expansion separates into even and odd powers: $\displaystyle e^{i\theta}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(i\theta)^n}{n!}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n\theta^{2n}}{(2n)!}+i\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n\theta^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$. $\blacksquare$
