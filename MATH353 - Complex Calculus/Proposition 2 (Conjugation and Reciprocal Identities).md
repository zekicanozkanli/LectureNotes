
> [!prop] [[Proposition 2 (Conjugation and Reciprocal Identities)]]: For $z,w\in\mathbb C$, $\overline{z+w}=\overline z+\overline w$, $\overline{zw}=\overline z\,\overline w$, $|\overline z|=|z|$, $\operatorname{Re}z=(z+\overline z)/2$, and $\operatorname{Im}z=(z-\overline z)/(2i)$. Also $z\overline z=|z|^2$; for $z\ne0$, $z^{-1}=\overline z/|z|^2$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* For $z=x+iy\ne0$, rationalizing the denominator gives $\displaystyle \frac1z=\frac1{x+iy}\frac{x-iy}{x-iy}=\frac{x-iy}{x^2+y^2}=\frac{x}{x^2+y^2}+i\frac{-y}{x^2+y^2}=\frac{\overline z}{z\overline z}=\frac{\overline z}{|z|^2}$. $\blacksquare$
