
> [!def] [[Definition 5 (Polar Form and Argument)]]: For $z\ne0$, write $z=r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)=re^{i\theta}$, where $r=|z|=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=(z\overline z)^{1/2}$. The angle $\theta$ is an **argument** of $z$. The **principal argument** $\operatorname{Arg}z$ is the argument satisfying $-\pi<\operatorname{Arg}z\leq\pi$; all arguments are $\arg z=\operatorname{Arg}z+2n\pi$, $n\in\mathbb Z$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: $1-i=\sqrt2\,e^{-i\pi/4}$; its point lies in the fourth quadrant with angle $-\pi/4$.
