
> [!cor] [[Corollary T12.1 (Polar Cauchy–Riemann Equations)]]: For $r>0$, the Cartesian equations in [[Theorem 12 (Cauchy–Riemann Necessary Conditions)]] become $u_r=\frac1r v_\theta$ and $\frac1r u_\theta=-v_r$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* By [[Proposition 7 (Polar Coordinate Partial Derivatives)]], the Cartesian equations $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$ take the forms $\displaystyle \cos\theta\,u_r-\frac{\sin\theta}{r}u_\theta=\sin\theta\,v_r+\frac{\cos\theta}{r}v_\theta$ and $\displaystyle \sin\theta\,u_r+\frac{\cos\theta}{r}u_\theta=-\cos\theta\,v_r+\frac{\sin\theta}{r}v_\theta$. Combining these equations gives $u_r=v_\theta/r$ and $u_\theta/r=-v_r$. $\blacksquare$
