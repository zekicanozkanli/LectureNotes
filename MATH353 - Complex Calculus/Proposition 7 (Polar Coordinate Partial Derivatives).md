
> [!prop] [[Proposition 7 (Polar Coordinate Partial Derivatives)]]: With $x=r\cos\theta$, $y=r\sin\theta$ and $r>0$, write $f=u(x,y)+iv(x,y)=u(r,\theta)+iv(r,\theta)$. Then $u_r=u_x\cos\theta+u_y\sin\theta$ and $u_\theta=-ru_x\sin\theta+ru_y\cos\theta$, and the same relations hold with $v$ replacing $u$. Conversely, $u_x=\cos\theta\,u_r-(\sin\theta/r)u_\theta$ and $u_y=\sin\theta\,u_r+(\cos\theta/r)u_\theta$, with the corresponding formulas for $v$.

> [!rem] The board expresses these transformations by a matrix and its inverse; the scalar formulas give the same coordinate change.
