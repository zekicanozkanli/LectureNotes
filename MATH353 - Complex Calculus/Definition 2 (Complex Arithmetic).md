
> [!def] [[Definition 2 (Complex Arithmetic)]]: For $z_1=x_1+iy_1$ and $z_2=x_2+iy_2$, **addition** and **multiplication** are defined by
> i. $z_1+z_2=(x_1+x_2)+i(y_1+y_2)$.
> ii. $z_1z_2=(x_1x_2-y_1y_2)+i(x_1y_2+x_2y_1)$.

> [!rem] Using $i^2=-1$, ordinary expansion gives $(x_1+iy_1)(x_2+iy_2)=x_1x_2+ix_1y_2+ix_2y_1+i^2y_1y_2$. Addition corresponds to vector addition in $\mathbb R^2$; the diagram represents $z_1+z_2$ by the parallelogram rule.
