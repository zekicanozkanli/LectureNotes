
> [!def] [[Definition 3 (Complex Modulus)]]: The **modulus** of $z=x+iy$ is $|z|=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$. The quantity $|z-w|$ is the distance between the points $z$ and $w$ in the complex plane.

> [!rem] The diagram represents $|z|$ as the length of the vector from the origin to $(x,y)$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The four loci are the half-plane $x<1$, the unit circle centered at $0$, the open disk of radius $1$ centered at $2i$, and the ellipse with foci $1$ and $2i$ and sum of distances $5$ (see [[Exercise 1 (Loci in the Complex Plane)]]).
