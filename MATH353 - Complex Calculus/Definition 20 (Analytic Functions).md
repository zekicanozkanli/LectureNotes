
> [!def] [[Definition 20 (Analytic Functions)]]: A function $f$ is **analytic**, or **holomorphic**, on an open set $S$ if it is differentiable at every point of $S$. It is **analytic at $z_0$** if it is differentiable throughout some neighborhood of $z_0$. A function analytic on all of $\mathbb C$ is called **entire**.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The function $f(z)=z^2$ is differentiable on $\mathbb C$, hence analytic on $\mathbb C$ and entire.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: The function $f(z)=|z|^2$ is differentiable at $0$ but is not analytic there (see [[Exercise 8 (Differentiability of Squared Modulus)]]).
