
> [!def] [[Definition 19 (Complex Derivative)]]: Let $f$ be defined in a neighborhood of $z_0$. Its **derivative** at $z_0$ is the limit, when it exists, $\displaystyle f'(z_0)=\lim_{z\to z_0}\frac{f(z)-f(z_0)}{z-z_0}=\lim_{\Delta z\to0}\frac{f(z_0+\Delta z)-f(z_0)}{\Delta z}$, where $\Delta z=z-z_0$. Other notation is $f'(z)=df/dz=dw/dz$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: For $f(z)=z^n$, $n\in\mathbb Z^+$, $f'(z)=nz^{n-1}$ (see [[Exercise 6 (Derivative of a Power)]]).

> [!eg] Ex. 2: The function $f(z)=\overline z$ is nowhere differentiable (see [[Exercise 7 (Differentiability of Conjugation)]]).

> [!eg] Ex. 3: The function $f(z)=|z|^2$ is differentiable only at $0$, where $f'(0)=0$ (see [[Exercise 8 (Differentiability of Squared Modulus)]]).
