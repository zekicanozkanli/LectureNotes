
> [!def] [[Definition 17 (Neighborhoods of Infinity)]]: A **neighborhood of infinity** is the exterior of a sufficiently large disk: $|z-0|>M=1/\varepsilon$, with small $\varepsilon>0$, together with the point $\infty$ in $\mathbb C^*$.

> [!rem] The sphere diagram shows a cap around the north pole corresponding to the exterior region in the complex plane.
