
> [!prop] [[Proposition 1 (Complex Numbers Form a Field)]]: $(\mathbb C,+,\cdot)$ is a field. The additive inverse of $z$ is $-z$, and the multiplicative inverse of a nonzero $z$ is $1/z$.
