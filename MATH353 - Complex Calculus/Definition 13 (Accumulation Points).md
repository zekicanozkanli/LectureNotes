
> [!def] [[Definition 13 (Accumulation Points)]]: A point $z_0$ is an **accumulation point**, or **limit point**, of $S$ if every deleted $\varepsilon$-neighborhood of $z_0$ contains a point of $S$:
> i. For every $\varepsilon>0$, there exists $z\in S$ with $0<|z-z_0|<\varepsilon$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: For $S=\{1/n:n\in\mathbb Z^+\}$, the only accumulation point is $0$. The diagram shows these points on the positive real axis approaching the origin.
