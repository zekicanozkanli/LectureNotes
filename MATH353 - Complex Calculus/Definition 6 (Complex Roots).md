
> [!def] [[Definition 6 (Complex Roots)]]: Let $n\in\mathbb Z^+$. A complex number $z$ is an **$n$th root** of $z_0$ if $z^n=z_0$. When $z_0=1$, it is an **$n$th root of unity**. If $z_0=r_0e^{i\theta_0}\ne0$ with $-\pi<\theta_0\leq\pi$, then $r_0^{1/n}e^{i\theta_0/n}$ is called its **principal root**.
