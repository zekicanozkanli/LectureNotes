
> [!def] [[Definition 8 (Open Sets)]]: A set $S\subseteq\mathbb C$ is **open** if every $z\in S$ has a neighborhood lying inside $S$:
> i. For every $z\in S$, there exists $\varepsilon>0$ such that $U_\varepsilon(z)\subseteq S$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The drawn region with its boundary omitted is open. The drawn square with a boundary edge included is not open: the marked boundary point has no disk lying inside the square.
