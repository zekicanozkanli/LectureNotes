
> [!thm] [[Theorem 4 (Uniqueness of Complex Limits)]]: If $\lim_{z\to z_0}f(z)$ exists, its value is unique.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Suppose the limit equals both $w_0$ and $w_1$. Taking $z$ sufficiently close to $z_0$ makes $\displaystyle |w_0-w_1|=|w_0-f(z)+f(z)-w_1|\leq |f(z)-w_0|+|f(z)-w_1|<\varepsilon$ for arbitrarily small $\varepsilon>0$. Thus $w_0=w_1$. $\blacksquare$
