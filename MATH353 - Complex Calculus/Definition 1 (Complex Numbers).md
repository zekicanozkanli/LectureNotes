
> [!def] [[Definition 1 (Complex Numbers)]]: A **complex number** has the form $z=x+iy$, where $x,y\in\mathbb R$ and $i^2=-1$. Its **real part** is $\operatorname{Re}z=x$ and its **imaginary part** is $\operatorname{Im}z=y$. The **complex plane** identifies $\mathbb C$ with $\mathbb R^2$ through $z\mapsto(x,y)$, with $i\mapsto(0,1)$.

> [!rem] If $\operatorname{Im}z=0$, then $z$ is real; if $\operatorname{Re}z=0$, then $z$ is purely imaginary.
