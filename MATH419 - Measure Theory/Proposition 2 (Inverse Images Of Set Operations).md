> [!prop] [[Proposition 2 (Inverse Images Of Set Operations)]]: Let $f:X\to Y$, $\{B_i:i\in I\}\subseteq\mathcal P(Y)$, and $B\subseteq Y$. Then:
> i. $f^{-1}(\bigcup_{i\in I}B_i)=\bigcup_{i\in I}f^{-1}(B_i)$.
> ii. $f^{-1}(\bigcap_{i\in I}B_i)=\bigcap_{i\in I}f^{-1}(B_i)$.
> iii. $f^{-1}(Y-B)=X-f^{-1}(B)$.
