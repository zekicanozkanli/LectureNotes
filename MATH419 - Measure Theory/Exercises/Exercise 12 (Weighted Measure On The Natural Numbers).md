# Exercise 12 (Weighted Measure On The Natural Numbers)

^nt-6358c2affd18bd12

###### Statement.

Let $X=\mathbb N$, $\mathcal A=\mathcal P(\mathbb N)$, and $(a_n)$ be nonnegative real numbers. Define
$$\mu(\emptyset)=0,\qquad \mu(E)=\sum_{n\in E}a_n\quad(E\ne\emptyset).$$
Show that $\mu$ is a measure. See [[Definition 10 (Measure)]] and [[Definition 1 (Extended Nonnegative Real Axis)]].
