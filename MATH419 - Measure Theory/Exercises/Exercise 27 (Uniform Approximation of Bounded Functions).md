
> [!ex] [[Exercise 27 (Uniform Approximation of Bounded Functions)]]: Let $f:X\to[0,\infty]$ be measurable and bounded: $f(x)\le K$ for some $K\in\mathbb R$ and every $x\in X$. Show that its constructed simple approximants $\varphi_n$ converge uniformly: for every $\epsilon>0$ there is $N\ge1$ such that $|f(x)-\varphi_n(x)|<\epsilon$ for every $n\ge N$ and every $x\in X$.
