# Exercise 9 (Disjointification Trick)

^nt-ff5214f9a57b1942

###### Statement.

Let $X$ be a set and $(A_k)$ a sequence of subsets. Define $E_1=A_1$ and $E_k=A_k-\bigcup_{i=1}^{k-1}A_i$ for $k>1$. Show that $(E_k)$ is disjoint and
$$\bigcup_{k=1}^\infty A_k=\bigcup_{k=1}^\infty E_k.$$
