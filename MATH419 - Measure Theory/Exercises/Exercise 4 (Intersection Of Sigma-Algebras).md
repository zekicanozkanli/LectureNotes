# Exercise 4 (Intersection Of Sigma-Algebras)

^nt-56a1dfd8a9ad3f9c

###### Statement.

Let $X$ be a set and $\{\mathcal A_i:i\in I\}$ a family of $\sigma$-algebras on $X$. Show that $\bigcap_{i\in I}\mathcal A_i$ is a $\sigma$-algebra. Use [[Definition 7 (Sigma-Algebra)]].
