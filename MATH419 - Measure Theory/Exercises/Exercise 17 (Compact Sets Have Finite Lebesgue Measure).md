# Exercise 17 (Compact Sets Have Finite Lebesgue Measure)

^nt-bb0a8da776a9281e

###### Statement.

For compact $K\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, show that $m(K)\ne\infty$. See [[Theorem 1 (Heine-Borel)]] and [[Proposition 11 (Cells Are Lebesgue Measurable)]].
