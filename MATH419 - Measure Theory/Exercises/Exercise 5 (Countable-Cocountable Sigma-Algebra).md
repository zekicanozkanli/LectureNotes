# Exercise 5 (Countable-Cocountable Sigma-Algebra)

^nt-1d75ab20edca5074

###### Statement.

For uncountable $X$, show that $\mathcal A=\{Y\subseteq X:Y\text{ is countable or }Y^c\text{ is countable}\}$ is a $\sigma$-algebra. Use [[Definition 7 (Sigma-Algebra)]].
