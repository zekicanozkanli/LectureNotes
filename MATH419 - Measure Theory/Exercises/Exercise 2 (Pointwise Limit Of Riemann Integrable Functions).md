# Exercise 2 (Pointwise Limit Of Riemann Integrable Functions)

^nt-a00c1fb08823fdd9

###### Statement.

Enumerate $[0,1]\cap\mathbb Q=\{r_1,r_2,\ldots\}$ and let $f_n$ be $1$ on $\{r_1,\ldots,r_n\}$ and $0$ elsewhere. Show that each $f_n$ is Riemann integrable and that $(f_n)$ converges pointwise to the Dirichlet function. Use [[Definition 6 (Riemann Integral)]].
