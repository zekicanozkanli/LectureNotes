# Exercise 13 (Restrict Outer Measure Covers To Small Cells)

^nt-f7fbf5e3784363bf

###### Statement.

Write a complete proof that, for each $\delta>0$, the definition of [[Definition 16 (Lebesgue Outer Measure)]] is unchanged when covers are restricted to cells with diameter $<\delta$. The source suggests subdividing every covering cell into finitely many smaller cells.
