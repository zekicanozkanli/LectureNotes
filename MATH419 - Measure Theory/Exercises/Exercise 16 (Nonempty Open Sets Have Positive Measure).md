# Exercise 16 (Nonempty Open Sets Have Positive Measure)

^nt-318aecf7cb4eaee2

###### Statement.

Let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be open. Show that $m(U)>0$ iff $U\ne\emptyset$. See [[Proposition 12 (Open Sets Are Countable Unions Of Open Cells)]] and [[Proposition 11 (Cells Are Lebesgue Measurable)]].
