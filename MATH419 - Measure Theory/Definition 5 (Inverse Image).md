> [!def] [[Definition 5 (Inverse Image)]]: If $X,Y$ are sets, $f:X\to Y$ is a function, and $B\subseteq Y$, the inverse image of $B$ is
> $$f^{-1}(B)=\{a\in X\mid f(x)\in B\}.$$

^nt-cc257747a7326e4e

###### Remarks.

The source uses $a$ as the bound variable and $x$ inside $f(x)$.
