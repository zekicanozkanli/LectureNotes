> [!prop] [[Proposition 1 (Interchange Of Nonnegative Double Series)]]: Let $\{a_{n,m}:(n,m)\in\mathbb N^+\times\mathbb N^+\}\subseteq[0,\infty]$. Then
> $$\sum_{(n,m)\in\mathbb N^+\times\mathbb N^+}a_{n,m}=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\sum_{m=1}^\infty a_{n,m}=\sum_{m=1}^\infty\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_{n,m}.$$
