> [!lem] [[Lemma 2 (Finite-Outer-Measure Test For Measurability)]]: A set $E\subset\mathbb R^n$ is measurable iff, for every $A\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ with $m^*(A)<\infty$,
> $$m^*(A)\ge m^*(A\cap E)+m^*(A\cap E^c).$$

^nt-bc7efea6d4aec072

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Since $A=(A\cap E)\sqcup(A\cap E^c)$, [[Proposition 6 (Monotonicity And Subadditivity Of Outer Measure)]] always gives the reverse inequality. When $m^*(A)=\infty$, equality in [[Definition 18 (Carathéodory Measurability)]] holds automatically. Thus it suffices to prove the stated inequality for finite outer measure.
