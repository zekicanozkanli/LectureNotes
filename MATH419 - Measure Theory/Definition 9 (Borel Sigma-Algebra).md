> [!def] [[Definition 9 (Borel Sigma-Algebra)]]: The $\sigma$-algebra generated by the open subsets of $\mathbb R^n$ is the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of $\mathbb R^n$, denoted $\mathcal B(\mathbb R^n)$.
