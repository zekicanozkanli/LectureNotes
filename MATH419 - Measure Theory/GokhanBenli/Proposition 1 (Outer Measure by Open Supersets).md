
> [!prop] [[Proposition 1 (Outer Measure by Open Supersets)]]: For every $A\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, $m^*(A)=\inf\{m(U):U\text{ is open and }A\subseteq U\}$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Let $\lambda(A)$ denote the infimum on the right. The definition of $m^*$ using open-cell covers gives $\lambda(A)\le m^*(A)$. If $U$ is open and $A\subseteq U$, then $m^*(A)\le m^*(U)=m(U)$, since $U$ is measurable. Taking the infimum yields $m^*(A)\le\lambda(A)$.$\blacksquare$
