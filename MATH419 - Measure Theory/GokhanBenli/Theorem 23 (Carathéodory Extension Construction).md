
> [!thm] [[Theorem 23 (Carathéodory Extension Construction)]]: Let $\mu$ be a pre-measure on an algebra $\mathcal A$ on $X$, and let $\mathcal A^*$ be the collection of subsets measurable for $\mu^*$. Then $\mathcal A^*$ is a $\sigma$-algebra containing $\mathcal A$, and the restriction of {$\mu^*$} to $\mathcal A^*$ is a measure extending $\mu$.

> [!rem] The source says “restriction of $\mu$ to $\mathcal A^*$” {the star in $\mu^*$ is reconstructed from the immediately preceding definition}. Its proof is referred to the analogous Lebesgue construction, without additional details.
