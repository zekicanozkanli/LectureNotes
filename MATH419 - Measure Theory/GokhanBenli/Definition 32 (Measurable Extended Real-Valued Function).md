
> [!def] [[Definition 32 (Measurable Extended Real-Valued Function)]]: A function $f:X\to\overline{\mathbb R}$ is **$\mathcal A$-measurable** if $f^{-1}((a,\infty])=\{x\in X:f(x)>a\}\in\mathcal A$ for every real $a$.

> [!rem] One may replace $(a,\infty]$ by $[a,\infty]$, $[-\infty,a]$, or $[-\infty,a)$ in the corresponding level-set tests. A real-valued measurable function remains measurable when viewed as extended real-valued. Also 
> $$
> f^{-1}(\{\infty\})=\bigcap_{k=1}^{\infty}f^{-1}((k,\infty]),\qquad f^{-1}(\{-\infty\})=\left(\bigcup_{k=1}^{\infty}f^{-1}((-k,\infty])\right)^c,
> $$
>  so both infinite-level sets are measurable.
