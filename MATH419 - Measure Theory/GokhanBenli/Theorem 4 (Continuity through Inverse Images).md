
> [!thm] [[Theorem 4 (Continuity through Inverse Images)]]: For $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$, the following are equivalent:
> i. $f$ is continuous.
> ii. $f^{-1}(U)$ is open for every open $U\subseteq\mathbb R$.
> iii. $f^{-1}((a,b))$ is open for every $a,b\in\mathbb R$.
