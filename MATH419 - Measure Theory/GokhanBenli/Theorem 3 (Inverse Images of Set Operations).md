
> [!thm] [[Theorem 3 (Inverse Images of Set Operations)]]: If $f:X\to Y$, $B\subseteq Y$, and $(B_i)_{i\in I}$ is a family of subsets of $Y$, then
> i. $f^{-1}(\bigcup_{i\in I}B_i)=\bigcup_{i\in I}f^{-1}(B_i)$.
> ii. $f^{-1}(\bigcap_{i\in I}B_i)=\bigcap_{i\in I}f^{-1}(B_i)$.
> iii. $f^{-1}(Y\setminus B)=X\setminus f^{-1}(B)$.
