
> [!def] [[Definition 28 (Abstract Carathéodory Measurability)]]: For the outer measure $\mu^*$ generated by a pre-measure on an algebra on $X$, a subset $E\subseteq X$ is **measurable** if $\mu^*(A)=\mu^*(A\cap E)+\mu^*(A\cap E^c)$ for every subset $A\subseteq X$.
