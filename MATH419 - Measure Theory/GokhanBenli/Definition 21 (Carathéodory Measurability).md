
> [!def] [[Definition 21 (Carathéodory Measurability)]]: A subset $E\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is **Lebesgue measurable** if 
> $$
> m^*(A)=m^*(A\cap E)+m^*(A\cap E^c)\quad\text{for every }A\subseteq\mathbb R^n.
> $$
>  This is the **Carathéodory condition**.

###### Motivation.
The aim is to identify *nonpathological* sets for measurement. Taking only $A=\mathbb R^n$ would require $m^*(\mathbb R^n)=m^*(E)+m^*(E^c)$; the condition instead requires $E$ to split *every* test set without losing outer measure. The lecture notes that Lebesgue originally used agreement of inner and outer measures, analogous to lower and upper Riemann sums, but follows the Carathéodory approach instead.
