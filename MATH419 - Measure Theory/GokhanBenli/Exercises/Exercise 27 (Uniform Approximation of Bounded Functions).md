
> [!ex] [[MATH419 - Measure Theory/GokhanBenli/Exercises/Exercise 27 (Uniform Approximation of Bounded Functions).md|Exercise 27 (Uniform Approximation of Bounded Functions)]]: Let $f:X\to[0,\infty]$ be measurable and bounded, so that $f(x)\le K$ for all $x$ for some real $K$. Show that the sequence $\varphi_n$ constructed in [[Theorem 26 (Approximation by Nonnegative Simple Functions)]] converges uniformly to $f$: for every $\epsilon>0$, there exists $N\ge1$ such that $|f(x)-\varphi_n(x)|<\epsilon$ for every $n\ge N$ and every $x\in X$.
