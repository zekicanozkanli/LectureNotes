> $$
> f^r(x)=\begin{cases}f(x),&-r\le f(x)\le r,\\r,&f(x)>r,\\-r,&f(x)<-r.\end{cases}
> $$
>  Show that $f^r$ is measurable. The integer-height versions are the truncations used in [[Lemma 10 (Products of Extended Measurable Functions)]].
