> ii. Show that $\sigma(\mathcal A)=\mathcal P(\mathbb Q)$.
> iii. Show that $\mu(\varnothing)=0$ and $\mu(A)=\infty$ for nonempty $A\in\mathcal A$ define a pre-measure.
> iv. Show that $\mu$ is not $\sigma$-finite.
> v. For each $k\ge0$, define $\mu_k(E)=k|E|$ for finite $E\subseteq\mathbb Q$ and $\mu_k(E)=\infty$ for infinite $E$. Show that $\mu_k$ is a measure.
> vi. Show that $\mu_k$ extends $\mu$.
