
> [!ex] [[Exercise 18 (The Left-Open Interval Algebra)]]: Let $\mathcal E_L$ consist of intervals $(a,b]$ and $(a,\infty)$ with $-\infty\le a\le b<\infty$. Show that $\varnothing\in\mathcal E_L$, that it is closed under finite intersections, and that the complement of any member is a finite disjoint union of members. Also show that the resulting algebra $\mathcal A_L$ of finite disjoint unions is not a $\sigma$-algebra.
