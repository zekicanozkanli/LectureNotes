
> [!def] [[Definition 30 (Characteristic Function)]]: For $E\subseteq X$, the **characteristic function** is $\chi_E:X\to\mathbb R$, with $\chi_E(x)=1$ for $x\in E$ and $\chi_E(x)=0$ for $x\notin E$.

> [!rem] When $E\in\mathcal A$, the source states that $\chi_E$ is measurable and prints 
> $$
> \chi_E^{-1}((a,\infty))=\begin{cases}X,&a\le0,\\E,&0<a<1,\\\varnothing,&a\ge1.\end{cases}
> $$
>  {unresolved: its placement of $a=0$ in the first branch conflicts with the strict inequality in the level set.}

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: For measurable $E$, $\chi_E$ is measurable, as asserted in the source.
