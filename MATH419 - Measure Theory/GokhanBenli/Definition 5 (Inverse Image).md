
> [!def] [[Definition 5 (Inverse Image)]]: For sets $X,Y$, a function $f:X\to Y$, and $B\subseteq Y$, the **inverse image** of $B$ is the set printed as 
> $$
> f^{-1}(B)=\{a\in X:f(x)\in B\}.
> $$
>  {unresolved: the bound variable $a$ does not match $x$ inside $f(x)$.}
