
> [!lem] [[Lemma 8 (Operations on Extended Measurable Functions)]]: If $f\in M(X,\mathcal A)$ and $c\in\mathbb R$, then $cf$, $f^2$, $|f|$, $f^+$, and $f^-$ belong to $M(X,\mathcal A)$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* The source applies [[Theorem 25 (Algebra of Real Measurable Functions)]] and [[Lemma 7 (Reduction to A Real-Valued Function)]]. For $c=0$, $0f=0$ by the convention $0(\pm\infty)=0$. For $c\ne0$, the infinite-level sets of $cf$ are the corresponding infinite-level sets of $f$, interchanged when $c<0$, and $g_{cf}=cg_f$; therefore $cf$ is measurable. For squaring, the source gives $(f^2)^{-1}(\{\infty\})=f^{-1}(\{\infty\})$ {unresolved: the negative-infinite level set is omitted}, $(f^2)^{-1}(\{-\infty\})=\varnothing$, and $g_{f^2}=(g_f)^2$, and concludes measurability. The remaining assertions are said to follow similarly; their arguments are not expanded in the source.$\blacksquare$
