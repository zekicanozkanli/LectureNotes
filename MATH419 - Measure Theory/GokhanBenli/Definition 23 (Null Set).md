
> [!def] [[Definition 23 (Null Set)]]: A subset $E\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is a **null set**, or **set of measure zero**, if $m^*(E)=0$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: Every countable subset of $\mathbb R^n$ is null. (see [[Exercise 16 (Countable Sets Are Null)]]).
