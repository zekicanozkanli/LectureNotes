
> [!def] [[Definition 26 (Pre-Measure)]]: Let $\mathcal A$ be an algebra on $X$. A **pre-measure** is a function $\mu:\mathcal A\to[0,\infty]$ satisfying
> i. $\mu(\varnothing)=0$.
> ii. If $(A_n)$ is disjoint in $\mathcal A$ and $\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n\in\mathcal A$, then $\mu(\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\mu(A_n)$.
