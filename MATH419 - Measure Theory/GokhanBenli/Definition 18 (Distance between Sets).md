
> [!def] [[Definition 18 (Distance between Sets)]]: The **distance** between subsets $A,B\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is $\operatorname{dist}(A,B)=\inf\{\|a-b\|:a\in A,\ b\in B\}$.
