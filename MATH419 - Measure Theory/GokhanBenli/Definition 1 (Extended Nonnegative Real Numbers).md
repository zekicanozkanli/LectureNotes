
> [!def] [[Definition 1 (Extended Nonnegative Real Numbers)]]: The **extended nonnegative real axis** is $[0,\infty]=[0,\infty)\cup\{\infty\}$, with the operations and order on $[0,\infty)$ extended by
> i. $x+\infty=\infty+x=\infty$ for $x\in[0,\infty]$.
> ii. $x\cdot\infty=\infty\cdot x=\infty$ for $x\in(0,\infty]$.
> iii. $0\cdot\infty=\infty\cdot0=0$.
> iv. $x<\infty$ for every $x\in[0,\infty)$.

If $a_n=\infty$ for every $n\ge k$, set $\lim_n a_n=\infty$. A nonnegative series has value $\infty$ if some term is $\infty$; otherwise it converges to a real number or diverges to $\infty$, regarded as convergence in $[0,\infty]$. Thus every nonnegative series is convergent in this extended sense.
For an arbitrary family $\{a_i:i\in I\}\subseteq[0,\infty]$, we define
$$
\sum_{i\in I}a_i=\sup \left\{\sum_{n \in F} a_n : F \subseteq I, \ F \text{ is finite} \right\}.
$$
