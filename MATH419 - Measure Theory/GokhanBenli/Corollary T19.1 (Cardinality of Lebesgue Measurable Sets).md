
> [!cor] [[Corollary T19.1 (Cardinality of Lebesgue Measurable Sets)]]: $\mathcal L(\mathbb R^n)$ has the same cardinality as $\mathcal P(\mathbb R^n)$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* The inclusion $\mathcal L(\mathbb R^n)\subseteq\mathcal P(\mathbb R^n)$ gives one cardinal bound. Let $C$ be an uncountable null set obtained from the Cantor construction in $\mathbb R^n$. Every subset of $C$ is measurable by [[Theorem 18 (Measurability of Null Sets)]], so $\mathcal P(C)\subseteq\mathcal L(\mathbb R^n)$. The source states that $\mathcal P(C)$ has the same cardinality as $\mathcal P(\mathbb R^n)$, giving the opposite bound.$\blacksquare$
