
> [!thm] [[Theorem 22 (Properties of Generated Outer Measure)]]: Let $\mu$ be a pre-measure on an algebra $\mathcal A$ on $X$. Then
> i. $E\subseteq F$ implies $\mu^*(E)\le\mu^*(F)$.
> ii. If $E\in\mathcal A$, then $\mu^*(E)=\mu(E)$.
> iii. For every sequence $(E_i)$ in $\mathcal P(X)$, $\mu^*(\bigcup_{i=1}^{\infty}E_i)\le\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\mu^*(E_i)$.

> [!rem] The lecture directs the reader to arguments analogous to those for $m^*$, without supplying the proofs.
