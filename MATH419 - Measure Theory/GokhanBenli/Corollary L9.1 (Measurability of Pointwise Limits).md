
> [!cor] [[Corollary L9.1 (Measurability of Pointwise Limits)]]: If $(f_n)$ is a sequence in $M(X,\mathcal A)$ converging pointwise to $f$, then $f\in M(X,\mathcal A)$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* For each $x$, $f(x)=\lim_n f_n(x)=\liminf_n f_n(x)$, so the result follows from [[Lemma 9 (Measurability of Pointwise Extrema)]].$\blacksquare$

###### Dependencies.
- [[Lemma 9 (Measurability of Pointwise Extrema)]]
