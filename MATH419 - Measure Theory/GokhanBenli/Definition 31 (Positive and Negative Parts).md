
> [!def] [[Definition 31 (Positive and Negative Parts)]]: For $f:X\to\mathbb R$, its **positive part** and **negative part** are the nonnegative functions $f^+(x)=\max\{f(x),0\}$ and $f^-(x)=\max\{-f(x),0\}$.

> [!rem] They satisfy $f=f^+-f^-$, $|f|=f^++f^-$, $f^+=(|f|+f)/2$, and $f^-=(|f|-f)/2$. Consequently $f$ is measurable if and only if both $f^+$ and $f^-$ are measurable.
