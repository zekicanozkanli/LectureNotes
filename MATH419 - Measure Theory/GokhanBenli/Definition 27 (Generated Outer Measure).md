
> [!def] [[Definition 27 (Generated Outer Measure)]]: If $\mu$ is a pre-measure on an algebra $\mathcal A$ on $X$, its **generated outer measure** is 
> $$
> \mu^*(E)=\inf\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\mu(E_i):E_i\in\mathcal A,\ E\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^{\infty}E_i\right\},\qquad E\subseteq X.
> $$
>  Its values lie in $[0,\infty]$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The length pre-measure generates $m^*$ and provides another construction of Lebesgue measure. (see [[Exercise 19 (Length Generates Lebesgue Outer Measure)]]).
