
> [!cor] [[Corollary L5.1 (Borel Sets Are Lebesgue Measurable)]]: $\mathcal B(\mathbb R^n)\subseteq\mathcal L(\mathbb R^n)$.

> [!rem] The source points ahead to the larger cardinality of the Lebesgue collection.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* By [[Lemma 5 (Open Sets as Countable Unions)]] and [[Theorem 12 (Measurability of Cells)]], every open subset is Lebesgue measurable. [[Definition 9 (Borel Sigma-Algebra)]] defines the smallest $\sigma$-algebra containing the open sets, so it is contained in the Lebesgue $\sigma$-algebra.$\blacksquare$
