
> [!def] [[Definition 22 (Lebesgue Sigma-Algebra and Measure)]]: The collection $\mathcal L(\mathbb R^n)$ is the **Lebesgue $\sigma$-algebra**. Its members are **Lebesgue measurable subsets**. The restriction $m=m^*|_{\mathcal L(\mathbb R^n)}$ is **Lebesgue measure**; in particular, $m(E)=m^*(E)$ for measurable $E$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: An open subset has positive Lebesgue measure if and only if it is nonempty. (see [[Exercise 17 (Positive Measure of Open Sets)]]).
