
> [!def] [[Definition 11 (Measure Space)]]: A **measure space** is a triple $(X,\mathcal A,\mu)$ consisting of a set $X$, a $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal A$ on $X$, and a measure $\mu$ on $\mathcal A$.
