
> [!def] [[Definition 8 (Generated Sigma-Algebra)]]: For $Y\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$, the **$\sigma$-algebra generated by $Y$** is $\sigma(Y)=\bigcap\{\mathcal A:\mathcal A\text{ is a }\sigma\text{-algebra on }X,\ Y\subseteq\mathcal A\}$.

> [!rem] The family being intersected is nonempty because it contains $\mathcal P(X)$. By [[Exercise 4 (Intersections of Sigma-Algebras)]], its intersection is a $\sigma$-algebra. It contains $Y$ and is contained in every $\sigma$-algebra containing $Y$, so it is the smallest such $\sigma$-algebra.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: $\sigma(Y)$ is contained in every $\sigma$-algebra containing $Y$. (see [[Exercise 21 (Minimality of Generated Sigma-Algebras)]]).
