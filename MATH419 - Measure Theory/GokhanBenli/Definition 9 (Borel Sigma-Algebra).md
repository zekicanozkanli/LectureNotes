
> [!def] [[Definition 9 (Borel Sigma-Algebra)]]: The **Borel $\sigma$-algebra** of $\mathbb R^n$, denoted $\mathcal B(\mathbb R^n)$, is the $\sigma$-algebra generated by the open subsets of $\mathbb R^n$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: $[0,1)$ is a Borel set. (see [[Exercise 6 (Half-Open Borel Interval)]]).

> [!eg] Ex. 2: Open balls generate $\mathcal B(\mathbb R^n)$. (see [[Exercise 7 (Open Balls Generate Borel Sets)]]).

> [!eg] Ex. 3: Closed subsets generate $\mathcal B(\mathbb R^n)$. (see [[Exercise 8 (Closed Sets Generate Borel Sets)]]).
