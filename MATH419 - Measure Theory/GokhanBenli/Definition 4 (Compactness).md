
> [!def] [[Definition 4 (Compactness)]]: A subset $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is **compact** if every open cover has a finite subcover: whenever $C\subseteq\bigcup_{i\in I}U_i$ with each $U_i$ open, there is a finite $J\subseteq I$ such that $C\subseteq\bigcup_{i\in J}U_i$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: Every compact subset of $\mathbb R^n$ has finite Lebesgue measure. (see [[Exercise 14 (Compact Sets Have Finite Measure)]]).
