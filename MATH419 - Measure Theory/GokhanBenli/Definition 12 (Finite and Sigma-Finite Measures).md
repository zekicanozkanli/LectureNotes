
> [!def] [[Definition 12 (Finite and Sigma-Finite Measures)]]: For a measure space $(X,\mathcal A,\mu)$, the measure is **finite** if $\mu(X)<\infty$, and **$\sigma$-finite** if there are $E_n\in\mathcal A$ with $\mu(E_n)<\infty$ for every $n$ and $X=\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}E_n$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: Every finite measure is $\sigma$-finite.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: Counting measure on $\mathbb N$ is $\sigma$-finite but not finite.
