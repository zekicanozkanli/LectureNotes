
> [!def] [[Definition 3 (Limit Inferior and Limit Superior)]]: For a sequence $(a_n)$ in $\overline{\mathbb R}$, the **limit inferior** and **limit superior** are 
> $$
> \liminf a_n=\sup_n\inf\{a_m:m\ge n\}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\inf\{a_m:m\ge n\},
> $$
>  
> $$
> \limsup a_n=\inf_n\sup\{a_m:m\ge n\}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sup\{a_m:m\ge n\}.
> $$

Every subset of $\overline{\mathbb R}$ has a unique supremum and infimum. The lecture states that the supremum is $\infty$ if and only if the set is not bounded above, and the infimum is $-\infty$ if and only if it is not bounded below.
