> [!def] [[Definition 3 (Limit Inferior And Limit Superior)]]: Let $(a_n)$ be a sequence in $\overline{\mathbb R}$. Define
> $$\liminf_n a_n=\sup_n\inf\{a_m:m\ge n\}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\inf\{a_m:m\ge n\},$$
> $$\limsup_n a_n=\inf_n\sup\{a_m:m\ge n\}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sup\{a_m:m\ge n\}.$$
