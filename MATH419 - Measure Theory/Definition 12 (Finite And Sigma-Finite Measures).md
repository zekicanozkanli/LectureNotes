> [!def] [[Definition 12 (Finite And Sigma-Finite Measures)]]: Let $(X,\mathcal A,\mu)$ be a measure space.
> i. $\mu$ is finite if $\mu(X)<\infty$.
> ii. $\mu$ is $\sigma$-finite if there is a sequence $E_1,E_2,\ldots\in\mathcal A$ such that $\mu(E_n)<\infty$ for every $n$ and $X=\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty E_n$.

^nt-292036ac974c93f6

###### Examples.

i. Every finite measure is $\sigma$-finite.
ii. The counting measure on $\mathbb N$ is $\sigma$-finite but not finite.
