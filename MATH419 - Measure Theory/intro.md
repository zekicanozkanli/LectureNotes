measure theory modern anlamda 1892 (Jordan measure) tanıtımı ile başladı. 1898'de Borel, Cantor'un set theory'sini kullanarak Jordan mesaure 'ın limitasyonlarını aşabilmesi için, he formulated a concept of measure that allowed for _countable_ operations paving the way for modern $\sigma$-algebras. Lebesgue 1902'de doktora tezinde Lebesgue measure ve Lebesgue integral kavramlarını tanıttı. 1914, Carathéodory extension theorem (and the concept of outer measures) provided the definitive, clean blueprint for constructing measures on abstract spaces. 1913-15 Radon,  extended the concepts of measures and integration to more generalized topological and abstract spaces. The final major piece of early measure theory development came from Soviet mathematician Andrey Kolmogorov. In 1933, he published his foundational book Foundations of the Theory of Probability. Kolmogorov realized that probability was just a special case of measure theory.

*Bartle,* after completing his doctorate, he taught at Yale University. By 1955, he moved to the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where he became the youngest person at the time to reach the rank of full professor at just 36 years old. While Bartle published over 60 scholarly articles in functional analysis and operator theory, his true legacy lies in his masterful textbook exposition. He possessed a rare talent for making dense, highly abstract mathematical concepts clear and accessible.
