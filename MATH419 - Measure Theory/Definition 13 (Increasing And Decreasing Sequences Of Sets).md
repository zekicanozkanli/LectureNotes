> [!def] [[Definition 13 (Increasing And Decreasing Sequences Of Sets)]]: Let $A_1,A_2,\ldots$ be subsets of a set.
> i. The sequence is increasing if $A_1\subseteq A_2\subseteq\cdots\subseteq A_n\subseteq A_{n+1}\subseteq\cdots$.
> ii. The sequence is decreasing if $A_1\supseteq A_2\supseteq\cdots\supseteq A_n\supseteq A_{n+1}\supseteq\cdots$.
