> [!def] [[Definition 2 (Extended Real Numbers)]]: The extended real numbers are $\overline{\mathbb R}=\mathbb R\cup\{\mp\infty\}=[-\infty,\infty]$, with algebra and order extended as follows:
> i. $-\infty<x<\infty$ for every $x\in\mathbb R$.
> ii. $x\pm\infty=\mp\infty$ for $x\in\mathbb R$; $\infty+\infty=\infty$ and $-\infty-\infty=-\infty$.
> iii. $x\cdot(\mp\infty)=\mp\infty$ for $x\in(0,\infty]$, and $x\cdot(\mp\infty)=\pm\infty$ for $x\in[-\infty,0)$.
> iv. $0\cdot(\mp\infty)=0$.

###### Remarks.

The signs in item ii follow the source. $\infty+(-\infty)=\infty-\infty$ is not defined.
Every subset of $\overline{\mathbb R}$ has a unique supremum and infimum. The source states that the supremum is $\infty$ iff the set is not bounded above, and the infimum is $-\infty$ iff it is not bounded below.
