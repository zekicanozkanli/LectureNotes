> [!prop] [[Proposition 15 (Null Sets And Their Subsets Are Measurable)]]: A null set is measurable. Every subset of a null set is measurable.

^nt-d1abd6fdfad47e53

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Suppose $m^*(E)=0$. For arbitrary $A$, [[Proposition 6 (Monotonicity And Subadditivity Of Outer Measure)]] gives $m^*(A\cap E)=0$ and $m^*(A)\ge m^*(A\cap E^c)=m^*(A\cap E^c)+m^*(A\cap E)$. Apply [[Lemma 2 (Finite-Outer-Measure Test For Measurability)]].
> If $F\subseteq E$, monotonicity gives $m^*(F)=0$, and the same argument makes $F$ measurable.

###### Remarks.

The source calls measures complete when subsets of null sets are measurable. Thus Lebesgue measure is complete.
