> [!def] [[Definition 11 (Measure Space)]]: Let $X$ be a set, $\mathcal A$ a $\sigma$-algebra on $X$, and $\mu$ a measure on $\mathcal A$. The triple $(X,\mathcal A,\mu)$ is a measure space.
