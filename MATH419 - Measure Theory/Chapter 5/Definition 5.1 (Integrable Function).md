> [!def] [[Definition 5.1 (Integrable Function)]]: The collection $L=L(X,\mathcal X,\mu)$ of integrable (or summable) functions consists of all real-valued $\mathcal X$-measurable functions $f$ defined on $X$, such that both the positive and negative parts $f^+,f^-$, of $f$ have finite integrals with respect to $\mu$. In this case, we define the integral of $f$ with respect to $\mu$ to be
> $$
> \int f\,d\mu=\int f^+\,d\mu-\int f^-\,d\mu.
> $$
> If $E$ belongs to $\mathcal X$, we define
> $$
> \int_E f\,d\mu=\int_E f^+\,d\mu-\int_E f^-\,d\mu.
> $$
