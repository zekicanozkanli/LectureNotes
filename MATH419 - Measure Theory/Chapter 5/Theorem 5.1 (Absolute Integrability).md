> [!thm] [[Theorem 5.1 (Absolute Integrability)]]: A measurable function $f$ belongs to $L$ if and only if $|f|$ belongs to $L$. In this case
> $$
> \left|\int f\,d\mu\right|\le\int|f|\,d\mu.
> $$

> [!cor] [[Corollary 5.1.1 (Integrability by Domination)]]: If $f$ is measurable, $g$ is integrable, and $|f|\le|g|$, then $f$ is integrable, and
> $$
> \int|f|\,d\mu\le\int|g|\,d\mu.
> $$
