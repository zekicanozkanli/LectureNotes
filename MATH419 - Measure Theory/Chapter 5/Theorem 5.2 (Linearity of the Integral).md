> [!thm] [[Theorem 5.2 (Linearity of the Integral)]]: A constant multiple $\alpha f$ and a sum $f+g$ of functions in $L$ belongs to $L$ and
> $$
> \int\alpha f\,d\mu=\alpha\int f\,d\mu,\qquad
> \int(f+g)\,d\mu=\int f\,d\mu+\int g\,d\mu.
> $$
