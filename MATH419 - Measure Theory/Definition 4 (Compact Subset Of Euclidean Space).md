> [!def] [[Definition 4 (Compact Subset Of Euclidean Space)]]: A subset $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is compact if every open cover of $C$ has a finite subcover: if $\{U_i:i\in I\}$ is a collection of open subsets of $\mathbb R^n$ with $C\subseteq\bigcup_{i\in I}U_i$, there is a finite $J\subseteq I$ such that $C\subseteq\bigcup_{i\in J}U_i$.
