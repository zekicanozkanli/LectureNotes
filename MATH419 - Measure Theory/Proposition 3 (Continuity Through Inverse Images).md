> [!prop] [[Proposition 3 (Continuity Through Inverse Images)]]: Let $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ be a function. The following are equivalent:
> i. $f$ is continuous.
> ii. $f^{-1}(U)$ is open for every open $U\subseteq\mathbb R$.
> iii. $f^{-1}((a,b))$ is open for every $a,b\in\mathbb R$.
