> [!prop] [[Proposition 9 (Translation Invariance Of Outer Measure)]]: For every $E\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ and $x\in\mathbb R^n$, $m^*(x+E)=m^*(E)$, where $x+E=\{x+e:e\in E\}$.

^nt-0aae006e7cb05380

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> If $I$ is a cell, $x+I$ is a cell with the same volume by [[Definition 15 (Cells Intervals And Volume In Euclidean Space)]]. Translate an $\epsilon$-approximate cell cover $(I_k)$ of $E$. By [[Definition 16 (Lebesgue Outer Measure)]],
> $$m^*(x+E)\le\sum_k\ell(x+I_k)=\sum_k\ell(I_k)\le m^*(E)+\epsilon.$$
> Thus $m^*(x+E)\le m^*(E)$. Translate back by $-x$ for the reverse inequality.
