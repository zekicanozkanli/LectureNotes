> [!def] [[Definition 2.6 (Truncation of an Extended Real-Valued Function)]]: If $n\in\mathbb N$, let $f_n$ be the “truncation of $f$” defined by
>
> $$
> f_n(x)=
> \begin{cases}
> f(x),&|f(x)|\le n,\\
> n,&f(x)>n,\\
> -n,&f(x)<-n.
> \end{cases}
> $$

> [!remark]
> Equivalently, $f_n$ clips the values of $f$ to the interval $[-n,n]$. In particular, $f_n$ is finite-valued even where $f$ equals $+\infty$ or $-\infty$.
