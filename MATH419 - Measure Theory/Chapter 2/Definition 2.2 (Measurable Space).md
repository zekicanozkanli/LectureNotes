> [!def] [[Definition 2.2 (Measurable Space)]]: An ordered pair $(X,\mathcal X)$ consisting of a set $X$ and a $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal X$ of subsets of $X$ is called a measurable space. Any set in $\mathcal X$ is called an $\mathcal X$-measurable set, but when the $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal X$ is fixed, the set will usually be said to be measurable.
