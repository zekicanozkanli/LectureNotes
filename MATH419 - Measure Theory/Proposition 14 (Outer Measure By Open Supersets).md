> [!prop] [[Proposition 14 (Outer Measure By Open Supersets)]]: For $A\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, $m^*(A)=\inf\{m(U):U\text{ is open and }A\subseteq U\}$.

^nt-5677bb41c6f6235b

###### Proof.

> [!proof]
> Let $\lambda(A)$ be the infimum on the right. Open-cell covers in [[Definition 16 (Lebesgue Outer Measure)]] give $\lambda(A)\le m^*(A)$. Conversely, every open $U\supseteq A$ is measurable by [[Proposition 12 (Open Sets Are Countable Unions Of Open Cells)#^nt-f70cc1d861352981|Corollary 1 (Borel Sets Are Lebesgue Measurable)]], and [[Proposition 6 (Monotonicity And Subadditivity Of Outer Measure)]] gives $m^*(A)\le m^*(U)=m(U)$. Taking the infimum gives $m^*(A)\le\lambda(A)$.
