> [!def] [[Definition 19 (Lebesgue Sigma-Algebra And Lebesgue Measure)]]: i. $\mathcal L(\mathbb R^n)$ is the Lebesgue $\sigma$-algebra on $\mathbb R^n$.
> ii. Its elements are the (Lebesgue) measurable subsets of $\mathbb R^n$.
> iii. $m=m^*|_{\mathcal L(\mathbb R^n)}$ is Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb R^n$.
