> [!def] [[Definition 4.3 (Integral of a Nonnegative Measurable Function)]]: If $f$ belongs to $M^+(X,\mathcal X)$, we define the integral of $f$ with respect to $\mu$ to be the extended real number
> $$
> \int f\,d\mu=\sup\int\varphi\,d\mu, \tag{4.3}
> $$
> where the supremum is extended over all simple functions $\varphi$ in $M^+(X,\mathcal X)$ satisfying $0\leq\varphi(x)\leq f(x)$ for all $x\in X$. If $f$ belongs to $M^+(X,\mathcal X)$ and $E$ belongs to $\mathcal X$, then $f\chi_E$ belongs to $M^+(X,\mathcal X)$ and we define the integral of $f$ over $E$ with respect to $\mu$ to be the extended real number
> $$
> \int_E f\,d\mu=\int f\chi_E\,d\mu. \tag{4.4}
> $$
