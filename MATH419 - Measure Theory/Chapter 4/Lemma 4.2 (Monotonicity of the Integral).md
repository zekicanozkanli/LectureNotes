> [!lem] [[Lemma 4.2 (Monotonicity of the Integral)]]: (a) If $f$ and $g$ belong to $M^+(X,\mathcal X)$ and $f\leq g$, then
> $$
> \int f\,d\mu\leq\int g\,d\mu. \tag{4.5}
> $$
> 
> (b) If $f$ belongs to $M^+(X,\mathcal X)$, if $E,F$ belong to $\mathcal X$, and if $E\subseteq F$, then
> $$
> \int_E f\,d\mu\leq\int_F f\,d\mu.
> $$

[1] Bartle, R. G. (1995). *The elements of integration and Lebesgue measure* (Wiley Classics Library ed., pp. 31). John Wiley & Sons.
