> [!lem] [[Lemma 4.3 (Fatou’s Lemma)]]: If $(f_n)$ belongs to $M^+(X,\mathcal X)$, then
> $$
> \int(\liminf f_n)\,d\mu\leq\liminf\int f_n\,d\mu. \tag{4.8}
> $$

> [!remark]
> Fatou's Lemma might fail if it is not assumed that $f_n\geq0$.$^{1}$
