> [!def] [[Definition 18 (Carathéodory Measurability)]]: A subset $E\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is (Lebesgue) measurable if
> $$m^*(A)=m^*(A\cap E)+m^*(A\cap E^c)\qquad\text{for every }A\subseteq\mathbb R^n.$$

^nt-747393fcbfbc3b2e

###### Motivation.

A measurable set splits every subset $A$ “nicely”: the outer measures of its two pieces add to that of $A$. Taking $A=\mathbb R^n$ alone would express $m^*(\mathbb R^n)=m^*(E)+m^*(E^c)$; the condition requires the equality for every subset.
The source attributes the criterion to C. Carathéodory. It contrasts it with Lebesgue's original inner/outer measure approach, analogous to lower/upper Riemann sums, which these notes do not develop.
