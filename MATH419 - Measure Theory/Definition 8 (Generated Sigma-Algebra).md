> [!def] [[Definition 8 (Generated Sigma-Algebra)]]: Let $X$ be a set and $Y\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$. The $\sigma$-algebra generated by $Y$, denoted $\sigma(Y)$, is the intersection of all $\sigma$-algebras containing $Y$:
> $$\sigma(Y)=\bigcap_{\substack{\mathcal A\text{ is a }\sigma\text{-algebra}\\Y\subseteq\mathcal A}}\mathcal A.$$

^nt-454883457485a6d1

###### Remarks.

$\mathcal P(X)$ is a $\sigma$-algebra containing $Y$, so the family being intersected is nonempty. [[Exercises/Exercise 4 (Intersection Of Sigma-Algebras)]] gives that $\sigma(Y)$ is a $\sigma$-algebra. It is contained in every $\sigma$-algebra containing $Y$.
