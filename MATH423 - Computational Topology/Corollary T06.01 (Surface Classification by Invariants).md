
> [!cor] [Corollary T06.01 (Surface Classification by Invariants)](Corollary%20T06.01%20%28Surface%20Classification%20by%20Invariants%29.md): Two closed connected surfaces are homeomorphic if and only if they have the same orientability and Euler characteristic. In particular, a closed connected surface with odd Euler characteristic is non-orientable.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: A closed connected surface with $\chi=1$ is nonorientable and homeomorphic to $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ (see [Exercise 17 (Orientability at Euler Characteristic One)](Exercises/Exercise%2017%20%28Orientability%20at%20Euler%20Characteristic%20One%29.md)).
