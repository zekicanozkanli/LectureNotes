
> [!thm] [Theorem 06 (Classification of Closed Surfaces)](Theorem%2006%20%28Classification%20of%20Closed%20Surfaces%29.md): Every closed connected surface is homeomorphic to exactly one of:  
> i. The sphere $S^2$.  
> ii. A connected sum of $g\geq1$ tori, orientable of genus $g$ and Euler characteristic $2-2g$.  
> iii. A connected sum of $k\geq1$ projective planes, non-orientable with Euler characteristic $2-k$.

> [!rem] Euler characteristic and orientability completely identify a closed connected surface and are computable from its triangle list. In general there is no algorithm deciding whether two triangulated four-dimensional manifolds are homeomorphic, as discussed in the source’s later Section 4. In higher dimensions computable invariants, particularly homology in Week 5, need not classify spaces.
