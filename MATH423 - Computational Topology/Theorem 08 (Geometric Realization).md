
> [!thm] [Theorem 08 (Geometric Realization)](Theorem%2008%20%28Geometric%20Realization%29.md): Every abstract simplicial complex of dimension $d$ admits a geometric realization in $\mathbb{R}^{2d+1}$.

> [!rem] The bound cannot be improved in general. For $d=1$, $K_5$ and $K_{3,3}$ cannot be realized in $\mathbb{R}^{2}$ (Kuratowski), while every graph can be realized in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ by placing vertices at distinct points of the moment curve $t\mapsto(t,t^2,t^3)$. No four such points are coplanar, so edges with four distinct endpoints do not meet. {The course treats finite complexes; the unrestricted infinite case in the source statement is unresolved.}
