
> [!def] [[Definition 1 (Topological Invariants)]]: **Topological invariants** are properties preserved under homeomorphisms.

###### Motivation.
Topology studies *qualitative properties* of spaces that remain unchanged under continuous deformations. A coffee mug and a doughnut can be deformed into one another without tearing or gluing and each has one “hole”; computational topology aims to make such holes precise and computable.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: Two critical invariants are [[Definition 2 (Connectedness)]] and [[Definition 5 (Compactness)]].
