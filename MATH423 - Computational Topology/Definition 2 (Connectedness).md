
> [!def] [[Definition 2 (Connectedness)]]: A space $X$ is **connected** if it cannot be written as the disjoint union of two nonempty open sets.

> [!rem] Every path-connected space is connected, but the converse fails.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: Intervals in $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, balls, and $S^n$ for $n\geq1$ are path-connected and hence connected.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: The decomposition $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}=(-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)$ shows that $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}$ is disconnected.

> [!eg] Ex. 3: The rational numbers are disconnected:
>
> $$
> \mathbb{Q}=\bigl(\mathbb{Q}\cap(-\infty,\sqrt{2})\bigr)\cup\bigl(\mathbb{Q}\cap(\sqrt{2},\infty)\bigr).
> $$
>

> [!eg] Ex. 4: The **topologist’s sine curve** $S=\{(0,0)\}\cup\{(x,\sin(1/x))\mid0<x\leq1\}$ is connected but not path-connected. No path reaches the origin from the graph because the graph oscillates infinitely often as $x\to0$.
