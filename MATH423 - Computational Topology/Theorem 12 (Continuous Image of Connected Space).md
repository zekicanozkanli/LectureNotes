
> [!thm] [Theorem 12 (Continuous Image of Connected Space)](Theorem%2012%20%28Continuous%20Image%20of%20Connected%20Space%29.md): The continuous image of a connected space is connected.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: A continuous image of a connected space is connected (see [Exercise 15 (Continuous Image of Connected Space)](Exercises/Exercise%2015%20%28Continuous%20Image%20of%20Connected%20Space%29.md)).

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* If $f(X)=U\cup V$ were a union of two disjoint nonempty relatively open sets, their preimages under the continuous map to the image would be disjoint nonempty open sets covering $X$, contrary to connectedness. $\blacksquare$
