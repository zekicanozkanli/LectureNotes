
> [!cor] [Corollary P08.01 (Full Simplices and Rips Cones)](Corollary%20P08.01%20%28Full%20Simplices%20and%20Rips%20Cones%29.md): A full simplex on a nonempty finite vertex set has the homology of a point over $\mathbb{Z}/2$. In particular this holds for $\operatorname{VR}_{\epsilon}(X)$ when all pairs have distance at most $2\epsilon$, or whenever some $x_0\in X$ is within $2\epsilon$ of every point of $X$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Any vertex is an apex of a full simplex. The pairwise condition gives a full simplex. Under the last condition, the flag property implies that adjoining $x_0$ to any Rips simplex still gives a simplex. Then apply [Proposition 08 (Homology of a Cone)](Proposition%2008%20%28Homology%20of%20a%20Cone%29.md). $\blacksquare$
