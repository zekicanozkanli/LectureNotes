
> [!def] [Definition 37 (Simplicial Cone)](Definition%2037%20%28Simplicial%20Cone%29.md): A simplicial complex $K$ is a **cone** with **apex** $v$ if $v$ is a vertex of $K$ and $\sigma\cup\{v\}\in K$ for every $\sigma\in K$.

> [!rem] Geometrically a cone contracts to its apex.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: A full simplex is a cone with any vertex as apex.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: The closed star of a vertex $v$ is a cone with apex $v$.

> [!eg] Ex. 3: The cone over $L$ is formed by adding a new vertex $v$ and the simplices $\sigma\cup\{v\}$ for $\sigma\in L$.

> [!eg] Ex. 4: The closed star is a cone and has the homology of a point; the link part is {unresolved} (see [Exercise 91 (Cones in Closed Stars)](Exercises/Exercise%2091%20%28Cones%20in%20Closed%20Stars%29.md)).

> [!eg] Ex. 5: The answers are false, true, and true (see [Exercise 97 (Cones and Planar Homology Tests)](Exercises/Exercise%2097%20%28Cones%20and%20Planar%20Homology%20Tests%29.md)).
