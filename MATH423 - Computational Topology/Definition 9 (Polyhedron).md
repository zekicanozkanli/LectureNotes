
> [!def] [[Definition 9 (Polyhedron)]]: The union of the simplices in $K$, denoted $|K|$, is the topological space called the **polyhedron** of $K$.

###### Motivation.
The complex $K$ is a *combinatorial object* suitable for data structures; $|K|$ is a *continuous topological space*. A space $X$, its concrete geometric model $|K|\cong X$, and the finite list of vertex sets $K$ are three levels of description. A computer stores the last.
