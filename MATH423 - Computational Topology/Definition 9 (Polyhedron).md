> [!def] [[Definition 9 (Polyhedron)]]: The union of all simplices in $\mathcal K$, denoted by $|\mathcal K|$, is a topological space called the polyhedron of $\mathcal K$.
###### Motivation.

The complex $\mathcal K$ is a combinatorial object (a set of simplices), which makes it suitable for combinatorial purposes. Its polyhedron $|\mathcal K|$ is a topological space.
