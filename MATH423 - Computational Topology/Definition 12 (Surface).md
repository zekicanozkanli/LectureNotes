
> [!def] [[Definition 12 (Surface)]]: A **surface** is a $2$-dimensional manifold.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The torus $T^2=S^1\times S^1$ is a surface: products of open intervals give open squares, which are homeomorphic to open disks.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: The double cone $x^2+y^2=z^2$, two cones glued at their tips, is not a surface. Removing the tip from a small connected neighborhood leaves two components, whereas a punctured open disk is connected.
