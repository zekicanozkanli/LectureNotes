
> [!def] [Definition 18 (Subspace Topology)](Definition%2018%20%28Subspace%20Topology%29.md): If $(X,\mathcal{T})$ is a topological space and $A\subseteq X$, the **subspace topology** on $A$ is $\mathcal{T}_{A}=\{U\cap A\mid U\in\mathcal{T}\}$.

###### Motivation.
Subspaces construct new spaces by taking *subsets* of an existing space. Openness depends on the ambient space.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: For $A=[0,1]\subseteq\mathbb{R}$, $[0,1/2)$ is not open in $\mathbb{R}$ but is open in $A$, since $[0,1/2)=(-1,1/2)\cap[0,1]$.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: The circle $S^1=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{2}\mid x^2+y^2=1\}$ inherits its topology from the plane. Open arcs are intersections with suitable open subsets of the plane.
