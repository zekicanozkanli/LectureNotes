
> [!lem] [Lemma 04 (Boundary Squared over Integers)](Lemma%2004%20%28Boundary%20Squared%20over%20Integers%29.md): For integral simplicial chains, $\partial_{k-1}\circ\partial_k=0$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Fix $i<j$ in $[v_0,\ldots,v_k]$. Deleting $v_i$ first puts $v_j$ in position $j-1$; the product of signs is $(-1)^{i+j-1}$. Deleting $v_j$ first leaves $v_i$ in position $i$, giving $(-1)^{i+j}$. The two copies of the same face cancel. $\blacksquare$
