
> [!def] [Definition 25 (Connected Sum)](Definition%2025%20%28Connected%20Sum%29.md): The **connected sum** $S_1\#S_2$ is formed by removing an open disk from each surface and gluing the resulting boundary circles together.

> [!rem] The sphere is an identity: $S\#S^2\cong S$, because removing a disk from $S^2$ leaves a disk that fills the removed disk in $S$. For words $w_1,w_2$ with disjoint letter sets, concatenation $w_1w_2$ presents the connected sum.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: $T^2\#T^2$ is the genus-$2$ surface, a sphere with two handles, with word $aba^{-1}b^{-1}cdc^{-1}d^{-1}$.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: $\mathbb{RP}^{2}\#\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ has word $aabb$ and is the Klein bottle.
