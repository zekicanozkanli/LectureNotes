
> [!def] [[Definition 13 (Manifold With Boundary)]]: Allowing surface points to have neighborhoods homeomorphic to the **half-disk** $\{(x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^{2}\mid x^2+y^2<1,\ y\geq0\}$ gives a **manifold with boundary**. The half-disk boundary corresponds to the surface boundary. Points with no open-disk neighborhood constitute the **boundary** $\partial S$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The closed disk $D^2$ has one boundary circle. In the earlier handwritten example its superscript was {unresolved}; the supplied typed notes explicitly specify the closed disk.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: The cylinder has two boundary circles.

> [!eg] Ex. 3: The Möbius band has one boundary circle.
