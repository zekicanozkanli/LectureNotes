> [!def] [[Definition 13 (Manifold With Boundary)]]: Allowing points of a surface to have neighborhoods homeomorphic to the half-disk, with the boundary of the half-disk corresponding to the boundary of the surface, gives a manifold with boundary.

^nt-8e765c9566f6e52f

###### Examples.

i. $D^2$, with superscript {unresolved} in the source.

ii. Möbius band.
