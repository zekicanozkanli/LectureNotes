
> [!thm] [Theorem 04 (Triangulation of Compact Surfaces)](Theorem%2004%20%28Triangulation%20of%20Compact%20Surfaces%29.md): Every compact surface admits a triangulation (Radó, 1925).

> [!rem] All topological $3$-manifolds are also triangulable (Moise, 1952). This fails in higher dimensions: the supplied notes cite Freedman’s $E_8$ $4$-manifold as non-triangulable, and Manolescu (2016) for non-triangulable manifolds in every dimension $n\geq5$. Such spaces cannot be represented by methods that start with a simplicial complex. Data-based spaces are built as complexes from the outset.
