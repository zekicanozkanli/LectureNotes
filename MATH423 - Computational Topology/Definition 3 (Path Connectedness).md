
> [!def] [[Definition 3 (Path Connectedness)]]: A space $X$ is **path-connected** if for every $x,y\in X$ there is a continuous map $\gamma:[0,1]\to X$ with $\gamma(0)=x$ and $\gamma(1)=y$.

> [!rem] Every path-connected space is connected; the converse fails, as illustrated by the topologist’s sine curve in [Definition 2 (Connectedness)](Definition%202%20%28Connectedness%29.md).

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: Intervals, $\mathbb{R}^{n}$, balls, and spheres $S^n$ for $n\geq1$ are path-connected.
