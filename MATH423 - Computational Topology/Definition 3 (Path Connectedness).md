> [!def] [[Definition 3 (Path Connectedness)]]: A space $X$ is path-connected if, for every pair of points $x,y\in X$, there exists a continuous map $\gamma:[0,1]\to X$ with $\gamma(0)=x$ and $\gamma(1)=y$.
