
> [!thm] [Theorem 07 (Dyck)](Theorem%2007%20%28Dyck%29.md): The connected sum of a torus and a projective plane satisfies
>
> $$
> T^2\#\mathbb{RP}^{2}\cong\mathbb{RP}^{2}\#\mathbb{RP}^{2}\#\mathbb{RP}^{2}.
> $$
>
> Consequently, a connected sum of $g$ tori and $k\geq1$ projective planes is homeomorphic to a connected sum of $2g+k$ projective planes.

> [!rem] Both sides are non-orientable with $\chi=-1$, consistent with [Corollary T06.01 (Surface Classification by Invariants)](Corollary%20T06.01%20%28Surface%20Classification%20by%20Invariants%29.md). Mixed connected sums therefore introduce no additional surface types.
