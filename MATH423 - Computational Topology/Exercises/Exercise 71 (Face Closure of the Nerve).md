
> [!ex] [Exercise 71 (Face Closure of the Nerve)](Exercise%2071%20%28Face%20Closure%20of%20the%20Nerve%29.md): Worksheet C6. Prove that the nerve of a finite collection of sets is an abstract simplicial complex.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Use the indices with $U_i\ne\emptyset$ as the vertices, so all vertex singletons are simplices. If $\emptyset\ne\tau\subseteq\sigma$ and $\sigma$ is in the nerve, then $\bigcap_{i\in\tau}U_i\supseteq\bigcap_{i\in\sigma}U_i\ne\emptyset$, so $\tau$ is also in the nerve. $\blacksquare$
