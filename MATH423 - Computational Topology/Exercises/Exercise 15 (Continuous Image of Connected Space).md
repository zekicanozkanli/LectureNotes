
> [!ex] [Exercise 15 (Continuous Image of Connected Space)](Exercise%2015%20%28Continuous%20Image%20of%20Connected%20Space%29.md): Worksheet A6. Decide whether the continuous image of a connected space is connected.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **True.** If $f(X)=U\cup V$ is a union of disjoint nonempty sets open in $f(X)$, the preimages $f^{-1}(U)$ and $f^{-1}(V)$ are disjoint nonempty open sets whose union is $X$, a contradiction. $\blacksquare$
