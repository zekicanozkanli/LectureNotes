
> [!ex] [Exercise 78 (Betti Numbers of Disjoint Unions)](Exercise%2078%20%28Betti%20Numbers%20of%20Disjoint%20Unions%29.md): Worksheet C13. Prove $b_k(K\sqcup L)=b_k(K)+b_k(L)$. Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* The chain groups of $K\sqcup L$ are the direct sums of the respective chain groups, and the boundary matrices are block diagonal. Thus ranks and dimensions of cycles and boundaries add, so the Betti numbers add. $\blacksquare$
