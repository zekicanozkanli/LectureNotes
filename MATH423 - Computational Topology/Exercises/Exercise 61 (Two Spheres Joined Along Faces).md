
> [!ex] [Exercise 61 (Two Spheres Joined Along Faces)](Exercise%2061%20%28Two%20Spheres%20Joined%20Along%20Faces%29.md): Worksheet B32. Find the Betti numbers of two tetrahedron boundaries glued (a) at a vertex, (b) along an edge. Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The counts are $(7,12,8)$ in (a) and $(6,11,8)$ in (b). Each has $\chi=3$ and is connected. In either gluing each sphere contributes its independent $2$-cycle and the boundary conditions on triangles are those of each sphere, so $b_2=2$. Thus $b_1=1+2-3=0$. $\blacksquare$
