
> [!ex] [Exercise 93 (Polygon Words and Dyck’s Theorem)](Exercise%2093%20%28Polygon%20Words%20and%20Dyck%E2%80%99s%20Theorem%29.md): Lecture exercise 9. **Outside Midterm 1.** Use boundary words to compute integral $H_1$ and the $\mathbb{Z}/2$ Betti numbers of (a) the sum of four projective planes with word $a_1a_1a_2a_2a_3a_3a_4a_4$, and (b) $aba^{-1}b^{-1}cc$, checking the vertex classes first. What does (b) say about $T^2\#\mathbb{RP}^2$?

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* (a) $H_1\cong\mathbb{Z}^3\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2$ and $b=(1,4,1)$. (b) There is one vertex class, $\chi=-1$, $H_1\cong\mathbb{Z}^2\oplus\mathbb{Z}/2$, and $b=(1,3,1)$. Thus $T^2\#\mathbb{RP}^2$ is the sum of three projective planes. $\blacksquare$
