
> [!ex] [Exercise 43 (Barycentric Coordinates in a Triangle)](Exercise%2043%20%28Barycentric%20Coordinates%20in%20a%20Triangle%29.md): Worksheet B14. Find the barycentric coordinates of $(1,1)$, $(3/2,3/2)$, and $(2,2)$ with respect to $(0,0),(3,0),(0,3)$. Classify the points as inside, on the boundary, or outside.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* From $x=(3t_1,3t_2)$ and $t_0=1-t_1-t_2$, the coordinates are $(1/3,1/3,1/3)$ for the interior barycenter, $(0,1/2,1/2)$ for the point on the edge between $(3,0)$ and $(0,3)$, and $(-1/3,2/3,2/3)$ for the exterior point. $\blacksquare$
