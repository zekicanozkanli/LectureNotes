
> [!ex] [Exercise 18 (Square Word abab)](Exercise%2018%20%28Square%20Word%20abab%29.md): Worksheet A9. Decide whether the square with boundary word $abab$ gives the Klein bottle.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **False.** The vertex classes are $\{P_0,P_2\}$ and $\{P_1,P_3\}$, so $\chi=2-2+1=1$, and the surface is $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$. The Klein bottle has word $aabb$ or $aba^{-1}b$. $\blacksquare$
