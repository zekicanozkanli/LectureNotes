
> [!ex] [Exercise 34 (Open Cover of Half-Open Interval)](Exercise%2034%20%28Open%20Cover%20of%20Half-Open%20Interval%29.md): Worksheet B5. Find an open cover of $[0,1)$ with no finite subcover and explain why Heine–Borel does not imply compactness here.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* For $n\geq2$, the sets $U_n=[0,1-1/n)$ are relatively open and cover $[0,1)$. A finite subcollection with largest index $N$ has union $U_N$, missing $1-1/(2N)$. The interval is bounded but not closed in $\mathbb{R}$. $\blacksquare$
