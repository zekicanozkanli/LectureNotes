
> [!ex] [Exercise 79 (Why Use Abstract Simplicial Complexes)](Exercise%2079%20%28Why%20Use%20Abstract%20Simplicial%20Complexes%29.md): Worksheet D1. Give two reasons to use simplicial complexes rather than graphs, and two reasons to prefer abstract to geometric complexes.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Graphs cannot distinguish a hollow from a filled triangle and cannot record group interactions such as a paper with three authors. Simplicial complexes capture both, and homology can detect holes in all dimensions. Abstract complexes are lists of integer sets, so they can be stored and checked exactly without floating point intersection tests. They also work for data with no coordinates, such as distance matrices, or spaces that do not embed in low dimensions. $\blacksquare$
