
> [!ex] [Exercise 68 (Circle and Line Nonhomeomorphism)](Exercise%2068%20%28Circle%20and%20Line%20Nonhomeomorphism%29.md): Worksheet C3. Prove that $S^1$ is not homeomorphic to $\mathbb{R}$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* If $f:S^1\to\mathbb{R}$ were a homeomorphism, deleting $p$ and $f(p)$ would give a homeomorphism of their complements. The punctured circle is a connected open arc, while $\mathbb{R}\setminus\{f(p)\}$ is a union of two disjoint nonempty open half-lines. Alternatively, $S^1$ is compact and $\mathbb{R}$ is not. $\blacksquare$
