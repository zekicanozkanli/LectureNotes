
> [!ex] [Exercise 67 (Continuous Image of Compact Space)](Exercise%2067%20%28Continuous%20Image%20of%20Compact%20Space%29.md): Worksheet C2. Prove that the continuous image of a compact space is compact.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Let $\{V_\alpha\}$ be an open cover of $f(X)$ by open subsets of $Y$, which may be chosen by [Definition 18 (Subspace Topology)](../Definition%2018%20%28Subspace%20Topology%29.md). The sets $f^{-1}(V_\alpha)$ are open and cover $X$. Compactness gives a finite subcover $f^{-1}(V_{\alpha_1}),\ldots,f^{-1}(V_{\alpha_n})$, so $V_{\alpha_1},\ldots,V_{\alpha_n}$ cover $f(X)$. $\blacksquare$
