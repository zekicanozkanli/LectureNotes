
> [!ex] [Exercise 35 (Three Hexagon Presentations)](Exercise%2035%20%28Three%20Hexagon%20Presentations%29.md): Worksheet B6. For each word, determine the vertex classes, Euler characteristic, orientability and surface: (a) $abcabc$; (b) $abca^{-1}b^{-1}c$; (c) $abcb^{-1}a^{-1}c^{-1}$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Label the corners $P_0,\ldots,P_5$. (a) The classes are $\{P_0,P_3\}$, $\{P_1,P_4\}$, and $\{P_2,P_5\}$. Thus $\chi=3-3+1=1$. Every letter repeats with the same sign and the gluing is antipodal, giving the nonorientable $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$. (b) All corners are identified and $\chi=1-3+1=-1$. The letter $c$ repeats with the same sign; the surface is the nonorientable sum of three projective planes. (c) The classes are $\{P_0,P_2,P_3,P_5\}$ and $\{P_1,P_4\}$. Thus $\chi=2-3+1=0$. Every letter appears with both signs, giving the orientable torus. $\blacksquare$
