
> [!ex] [Exercise 72 (Čech Inclusion in Metric Spaces)](Exercise%2072%20%28%C4%8Cech%20Inclusion%20in%20Metric%20Spaces%29.md): Worksheet C7. Prove $\check{C}_\epsilon(X)\subseteq\operatorname{VR}_\epsilon(X)$ for every finite subset $X$ of a metric space.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* If $\sigma\in\check{C}_\epsilon(X)$, choose a point $z$ with $d(x,z)\leq\epsilon$ for every $x\in\sigma$. For $x,y\in\sigma$, $d(x,y)\leq d(x,z)+d(z,y)\leq2\epsilon$, so $\sigma\in\operatorname{VR}_\epsilon(X)$. $\blacksquare$
