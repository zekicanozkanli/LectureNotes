
> [!ex] [Exercise 94 (Homology of a Hexagonal Torus)](Exercise%2094%20%28Homology%20of%20a%20Hexagonal%20Torus%29.md): Lecture exercise 10. For $abca^{-1}b^{-1}c^{-1}$, find the vertex classes, $\chi$, the surface, and its Betti numbers over $\mathbb{Z}/2$. **Outside Midterm 1:** why can [Proposition 10 (First Homology from Polygon Words)](../Proposition%2010%20%28First%20Homology%20from%20Polygon%20Words%29.md) not be applied directly?

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The classes are $\{P_0,P_2,P_4\}$ and $\{P_1,P_3,P_5\}$, giving $\chi=2-3+1=0$. The orientable surface is a torus with $b=(1,2,1)$. The one-vertex hypothesis fails; the true integral $H_1$ is $\mathbb{Z}^2$, while the naive recipe would give $\mathbb{Z}^3$. $\blacksquare$
