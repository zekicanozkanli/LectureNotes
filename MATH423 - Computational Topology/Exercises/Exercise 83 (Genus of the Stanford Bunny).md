
> [!ex] [Exercise 83 (Genus of the Stanford Bunny)](Exercise%2083%20%28Genus%20of%20the%20Stanford%20Bunny%29.md): Worksheet D5. The connected orientable Stanford bunny mesh has $V=34{,}834$, $E=104{,}288$, $F=69{,}451$, and $B=223$ boundary edges in five loops. Verify $3F=2E-B$, compute $\chi$ and the genus, and find $\chi$ after capping every loop with a cone.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* $3\cdot69{,}451=208{,}353=2\cdot104{,}288-223$. Also $\chi=34{,}834-104{,}288+69{,}451=-3=2-2g-5$, giving $g=0$. Capping adds five vertices, $223$ edges and $223$ triangles. Thus $\chi$ becomes $2$ and the capped surface is a sphere. $\blacksquare$
