
> [!ex] [Exercise 92 (Attaching Hollow and Solid Triangles)](Exercise%2092%20%28Attaching%20Hollow%20and%20Solid%20Triangles%29.md): Lecture exercise 8. How do the Betti numbers over $\mathbb{Z}/2$ of $L$ change if a hollow triangle is attached at one vertex? What if a solid triangle is attached instead?

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The hollow triangle increases $b_1$ by one and leaves the other Betti numbers unchanged. Attaching a solid triangle changes none. $\blacksquare$
