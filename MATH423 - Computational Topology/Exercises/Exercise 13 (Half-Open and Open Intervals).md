
> [!ex] [Exercise 13 (Half-Open and Open Intervals)](Exercise%2013%20%28Half-Open%20and%20Open%20Intervals%29.md): Worksheet A4. Decide whether $[0,1)$ and $(0,1)$ are homeomorphic.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **False.** Removing $0$ from $[0,1)$ leaves a connected space. Removing any $p$ from $(0,1)$ leaves the disconnected space $(0,p)\cup(p,1)$. A homeomorphism $f$ would restrict to a homeomorphism $[0,1)\setminus\{0\}\to(0,1)\setminus\{f(0)\}$. $\blacksquare$
