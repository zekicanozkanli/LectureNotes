
> [!ex] [Exercise 49 (Equilateral Triangle at Scale 1.1)](Exercise%2049%20%28Equilateral%20Triangle%20at%20Scale%201.1%29.md): Worksheet B20. Let $X$ be an equilateral triangle of side $2$. Describe $\check{C}_{1.1}(X)$ and $\operatorname{VR}_{1.1}(X)$ and verify $\check{C}_{1.1}(X)\subseteq\operatorname{VR}_{1.1}(X)\subseteq\check{C}_{1.1\sqrt{2}}(X)$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Every pair distance is $2\leq2.2$, so the Vietoris-Rips triangle is filled and every pair of Čech disks meets. The circumradius $2/\sqrt{3}\approx1.155$ exceeds $1.1$, so the triple intersection is empty. Since $1.1\sqrt{2}\approx1.556>2/\sqrt{3}$, the enlarged Čech triangle is filled. $\blacksquare$
