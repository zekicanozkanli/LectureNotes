
> [!ex] [Exercise 01 (Maps from Discrete Spaces)](Exercise%2001%20%28Maps%20from%20Discrete%20Spaces%29.md): Let $X$ have the discrete topology and let $Y$ be any topological space. Prove that every function $f:X\to Y$ is continuous.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Let $f:X\to Y$ be arbitrary and let $V$ be open in $Y$. Since $X$ has the discrete topology, every subset of $X$ is open. In particular, $f^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$. By [Definition 20 (Continuous Map)](../Definition%2020%20%28Continuous%20Map%29.md), $f$ is continuous. $\blacksquare$
