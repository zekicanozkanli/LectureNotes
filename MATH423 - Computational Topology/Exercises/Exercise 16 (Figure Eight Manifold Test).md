
> [!ex] [Exercise 16 (Figure Eight Manifold Test)](Exercise%2016%20%28Figure%20Eight%20Manifold%20Test%29.md): Worksheet A7. Decide whether two circles joined at one point form a $1$-manifold.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **False.** Deleting the crossing from a small connected neighborhood leaves four components, whereas deleting a point from an open interval leaves two. $\blacksquare$
