
> [!ex] [Exercise 96 (Tetrahedron Boundary with an Edge)](Exercise%2096%20%28Tetrahedron%20Boundary%20with%20an%20Edge%29.md): Lecture exercise 12. Attach the edge $\{1,5\}$ to vertex $1$ of a tetrahedron boundary, with $5$ a new vertex. Find the Betti numbers over $\mathbb{Z}/2$ and $\operatorname{lk}(1)$. **Outside Midterm 1:** is its polyhedron a surface?

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The complex has $b=(1,0,1)$. The link of $1$ is a hollow triangle and an isolated vertex $5$, giving link tuple $(2,1)$. The polyhedron is not a surface. $\blacksquare$
