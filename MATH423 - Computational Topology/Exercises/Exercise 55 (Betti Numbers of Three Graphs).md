
> [!ex] [Exercise 55 (Betti Numbers of Three Graphs)](Exercise%2055%20%28Betti%20Numbers%20of%20Three%20Graphs%29.md): Worksheet B26. Compute the Betti numbers of (a) the tetrahedron’s $1$-skeleton, (b) $K_5$, and (c) the octahedron’s $1$-skeleton. Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* All are connected graphs, so $b_0=1$ and $b_1=E-V+1$. The three values of $b_1$ are $6-4+1=3$, $10-5+1=6$, and $12-6+1=7$. $\blacksquare$
