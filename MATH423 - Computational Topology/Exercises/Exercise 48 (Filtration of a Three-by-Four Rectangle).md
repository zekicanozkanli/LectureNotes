
> [!ex] [Exercise 48 (Filtration of a Three-by-Four Rectangle)](Exercise%2048%20%28Filtration%20of%20a%20Three-by-Four%20Rectangle%29.md): Worksheet B19. For $X=\{(0,0),(4,0),(4,3),(0,3)\}$, describe both complexes for all $\epsilon>0$ and find the scales at which they are homotopy equivalent to a circle.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The side lengths are $3,4$ and the diagonals have length $5$. Each triple makes a right triangle with enclosing radius $5/2$, and the ball centered at $(2,3/2)$ of radius $5/2$ encloses all four. Both complexes are four points for $\epsilon<3/2$, two disjoint edges for $3/2\leq\epsilon<2$, a hollow rectangle for $2\leq\epsilon<5/2$, and the full $3$-simplex for $\epsilon\geq5/2$. $\blacksquare$
