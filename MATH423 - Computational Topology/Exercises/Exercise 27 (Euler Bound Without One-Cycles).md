
> [!ex] [Exercise 27 (Euler Bound Without One-Cycles)](Exercise%2027%20%28Euler%20Bound%20Without%20One-Cycles%29.md): Worksheet A18. Decide whether a nonempty complex of dimension at most $2$ with $b_1=0$ has $\chi\geq1$. Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **True.** Euler–Poincaré gives $\chi=b_0-b_1+b_2=b_0+b_2\geq b_0\geq1$. $\blacksquare$
