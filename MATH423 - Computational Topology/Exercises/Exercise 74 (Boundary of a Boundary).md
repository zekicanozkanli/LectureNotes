
> [!ex] [Exercise 74 (Boundary of a Boundary)](Exercise%2074%20%28Boundary%20of%20a%20Boundary%29.md): Worksheet C9. Prove $\partial_{k-1}\circ\partial_k=0$ over $\mathbb{Z}/2$ and deduce $B_k\subseteq Z_k$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* By linearity check one simplex. Each face obtained by deleting two vertices occurs twice, once in each deletion order, and its coefficient is $1+1=0$. Thus $\partial_{k-1}\partial_k=0$. For $c=\partial_{k+1}d$, this gives $\partial_k c=0$. $\blacksquare$
