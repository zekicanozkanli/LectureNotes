
> [!ex] [Exercise 50 (Vietoris-Rips Filtration of a Hexagon)](Exercise%2050%20%28Vietoris-Rips%20Filtration%20of%20a%20Hexagon%29.md): Worksheet B21. For a regular hexagon of side $1$, describe $\operatorname{VR}_\epsilon$ and give its $f$-vector at $\epsilon=0.4,0.6,0.9,1$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Distances are $1,\sqrt{3},2$. At $0.4$ there are six points, $f=(6)$. At $0.6$ there is a hexagonal cycle, $f=(6,6)$. At $0.9$ all pairs except opposite pairs are joined, and all simplices avoid opposite pairs, giving the octahedron boundary, $f=(6,12,8)$. At $1$ the complex is the full $5$-simplex, $f=(6,15,20,15,6,1)$. $\blacksquare$
