###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **True.** The map $t\mapsto\tan(\pi(t-1/2))$ is a homeomorphism. Alternatively compose $t\mapsto2t-1$ with $s\mapsto s/(1-\lvert s\rvert)$. $\blacksquare$
