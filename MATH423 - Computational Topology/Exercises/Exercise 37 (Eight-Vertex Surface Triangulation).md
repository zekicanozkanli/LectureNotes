
> [!ex] [Exercise 37 (Eight-Vertex Surface Triangulation)](Exercise%2037%20%28Eight-Vertex%20Surface%20Triangulation%29.md): Worksheet B8. A closed connected surface triangulation has $8$ vertices and $16$ triangles. Find the number of edges and identify the possible surfaces.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* $E=3F/2=24$, so $\chi=8-24+16=0$. By classification, the surface is a torus or a Klein bottle. $\blacksquare$
