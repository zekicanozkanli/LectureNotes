
> [!ex] [Exercise 81 (Link Condition for Edge Contraction)](Exercise%2081%20%28Link%20Condition%20for%20Edge%20Contraction%29.md): Worksheet D3. Check $\operatorname{lk}(a)\cap\operatorname{lk}(b)=\operatorname{lk}(\{a,b\})$ for an edge in the octahedron and one in the tetrahedron, and interpret the result for contraction.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* For the octahedron edge $\{N,1\}$, both sides consist of vertices $2,4$. The condition holds and the edge contracts to give a five-vertex sphere. For tetrahedron edge $\{1,2\}$, the intersection contains the edge $\{3,4\}$, whereas the edge link consists only of the vertices $3,4$. Contraction would merge two triangles and destroy the surface. $\blacksquare$
