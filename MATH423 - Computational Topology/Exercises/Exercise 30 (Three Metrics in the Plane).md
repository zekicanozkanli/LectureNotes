###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The absolute coordinate differences are $3,4$, so $d_1=7$, $d_2=5$, $d_\infty=4$, and $4\leq5\leq7\leq5\sqrt{2}\approx7.07$. $\blacksquare$
