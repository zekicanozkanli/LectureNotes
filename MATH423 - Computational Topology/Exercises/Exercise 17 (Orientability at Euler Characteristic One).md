
> [!ex] [Exercise 17 (Orientability at Euler Characteristic One)](Exercise%2017%20%28Orientability%20at%20Euler%20Characteristic%20One%29.md): Worksheet A8. Decide whether a closed connected surface with $\chi=1$ can be orientable.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **False.** A closed connected orientable surface has $\chi=2-2g$, which is even. A closed connected surface with $\chi=1$ is $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$. $\blacksquare$
