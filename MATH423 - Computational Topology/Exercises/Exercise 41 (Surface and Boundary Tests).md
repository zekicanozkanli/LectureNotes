
> [!ex] [Exercise 41 (Surface and Boundary Tests)](Exercise%2041%20%28Surface%20and%20Boundary%20Tests%29.md): Worksheet B12. Decide which spaces are surfaces, possibly with boundary: a closed disk; two closed disks glued at one boundary point; the union of the $xy$-plane and the $z$-axis in $\mathbb{R}^3$; and $S^1\times(0,1)$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The closed disk is a surface with boundary. The two joined disks are not: deleting the common point disconnects a small neighborhood, so it cannot be an open disk or half-disk neighborhood. The plane with the axis is not a surface; points on the axis away from the origin have small interval neighborhoods rather than disk neighborhoods. The open cylinder is a surface without boundary. $\blacksquare$
