
> [!ex] [Exercise 63 (Betti Numbers of Hexagonal Rips)](Exercise%2063%20%28Betti%20Numbers%20of%20Hexagonal%20Rips%29.md): Worksheet B34. For the hexagon in [Exercise 50 (Vietoris-Rips Filtration of a Hexagon)](Exercise%2050%20%28Vietoris-Rips%20Filtration%20of%20a%20Hexagon%29.md), find the Vietoris-Rips Betti numbers at $\epsilon=0.6,0.9,1$. Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* At $0.6$ the complex is the hexagonal cycle, giving $(1,1)$. At $0.9$ it is the octahedron boundary, giving $(1,0,1)$ by [Proposition 03 (Betti Numbers of Closed Surfaces)](../Proposition%2003%20%28Betti%20Numbers%20of%20Closed%20Surfaces%29.md) and $\chi=2$. At $1$ the full $5$-simplex gives $(1,0,0,0,0,0)$. $\blacksquare$
