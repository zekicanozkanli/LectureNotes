###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **True.** Since $\partial_0=0$, $b_0=n_0-\operatorname{rank}\partial_1$. By [Theorem 11 (Zeroth Homology Counts Components)](../Theorem%2011%20%28Zeroth%20Homology%20Counts%20Components%29.md), $b_0=1$. $\blacksquare$
