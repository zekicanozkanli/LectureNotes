
> [!ex] [Exercise 44 (Completing a Simplicial Complex)](Exercise%2044%20%28Completing%20a%20Simplicial%20Complex%29.md): Worksheet B15. Is $\{\{1\},\{2\},\{3\},\{4\},\{1,2\},\{2,3\},\{1,2,3\}\}$ an abstract simplicial complex? If not, find the smallest complex containing it.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The face $\{1,3\}$ of $\{1,2,3\}$ is missing. Adding it gives a triangle with all its faces together with the isolated vertex $4$. $\blacksquare$
