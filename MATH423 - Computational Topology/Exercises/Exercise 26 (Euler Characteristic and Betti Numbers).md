Decide whether two finite simplicial complexes with the same Euler characteristic have the same Betti numbers. Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **False.** The tetrahedron boundary and two isolated points both have $\chi=2$, but their Betti tuples are $(1,0,1)$ and $(2)$. $\blacksquare$
