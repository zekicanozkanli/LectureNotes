
> [!ex] [Exercise 76 (Proving the Euler-Poincaré Formula)](Exercise%2076%20%28Proving%20the%20Euler-Poincar%C3%A9%20Formula%29.md): Worksheet C11. Prove $\sum_k(-1)^k n_k=\sum_k(-1)^k b_k$. Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Write $r_k=\operatorname{rank}\partial_k$, with $r_0=r_{d+1}=0$. Rank–nullity gives $\dim Z_k=n_k-r_k$ and $\dim B_k=r_{k+1}$, so $b_k=n_k-r_k-r_{k+1}$. Each interior rank occurs twice with opposite signs in the alternating sum and the endpoint ranks are zero. $\blacksquare$
