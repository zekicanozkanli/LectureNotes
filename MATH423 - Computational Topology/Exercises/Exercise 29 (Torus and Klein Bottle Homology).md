
> [!ex] [Exercise 29 (Torus and Klein Bottle Homology)](Exercise%2029%20%28Torus%20and%20Klein%20Bottle%20Homology%29.md): Worksheet A20. Decide whether the torus and Klein bottle have the same Betti numbers over $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **True.** Both are closed connected surfaces with $\chi=0$, so [Proposition 03 (Betti Numbers of Closed Surfaces)](../Proposition%2003%20%28Betti%20Numbers%20of%20Closed%20Surfaces%29.md) gives $(1,2,1)$ for both. Integer coefficients distinguish them, beyond the stated coverage. $\blacksquare$
