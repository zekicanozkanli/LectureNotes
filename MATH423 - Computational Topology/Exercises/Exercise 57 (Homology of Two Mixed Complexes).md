
> [!ex] [Exercise 57 (Homology of Two Mixed Complexes)](Exercise%2057%20%28Homology%20of%20Two%20Mixed%20Complexes%29.md): Worksheet B28. Find the Betti numbers of the complex with maximal simplices $\{1,2,3\},\{3,4\},\{4,5\}$ and of the complex in [Exercise 45 (Counting Simplices Stars and Links)](Exercise%2045%20%28Counting%20Simplices%20Stars%20and%20Links%29.md). Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The first has $n=(5,5,1)$ and ranks $4,1$ for $\partial_1,\partial_2$, giving $(1,0,0)$; it deformation retracts to a point. The second has $n=(6,8,2)$ and ranks $5,2$, since its triangle boundaries are independent. Its tuple is $(1,1,0)$, with the hollow triangle on $4,5,6$ giving the hole. Its Euler characteristic is $0=1-1+0$. $\blacksquare$
