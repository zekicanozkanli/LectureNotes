
> [!ex] [Exercise 42 (Cycles in Vertex Links)](Exercise%2042%20%28Cycles%20in%20Vertex%20Links%29.md): Worksheet B13. Explain why the link of every vertex of a closed surface triangulation is a cycle with at least three vertices and why every vertex has degree at least three.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* For $w$ in the link of $v$, the edge $vw$ lies in exactly two triangles, so $w$ has degree two in the link. Using the connectedness assertion posed in [Exercise 06 (Vertex Links in Closed Surfaces)](Exercise%2006%20%28Vertex%20Links%20in%20Closed%20Surfaces%29.md), the link is connected, hence is a single cycle. A simplicial cycle has at least three vertices since a pair spans at most one edge. The degree of $v$ counts the vertices of its link. $\blacksquare$
