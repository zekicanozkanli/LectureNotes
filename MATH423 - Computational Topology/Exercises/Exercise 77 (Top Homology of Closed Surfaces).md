
> [!ex] [Exercise 77 (Top Homology of Closed Surfaces)](Exercise%2077%20%28Top%20Homology%20of%20Closed%20Surfaces%29.md): Worksheet C12. Prove that a triangulated closed connected surface has $b_2=1$ over $\mathbb{Z}/2$, and deduce $b_1=2-\chi$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* There are no $3$-simplices, so $H_2=Z_2$. A $2$-cycle is a set of triangles meeting each edge evenly. Since each edge belongs to two triangles, the presence of one triangle forces all of its neighbors across its edges, and connectedness propagates this to all triangles. The only cycles are zero and the sum of all triangles, which is a cycle. Thus $b_2=1$; with $b_0=1$, Euler–Poincaré gives $b_1=2-\chi$. $\blacksquare$
