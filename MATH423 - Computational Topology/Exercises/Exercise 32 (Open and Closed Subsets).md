
> [!ex] [Exercise 32 (Open and Closed Subsets)](Exercise%2032%20%28Open%20and%20Closed%20Subsets%29.md): Worksheet B3. In $X=[0,2]$ with the real subspace topology, classify $[0,1)$, $(1,2]$, $\{1\}$, $(1/2,3/2)$, and $X$ as open, closed, both, or neither.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The sets $[0,1)=(-1,1)\cap X$ and $(1,2]=(1,3)\cap X$ are open but not closed. The singleton $\{1\}$ is closed but not open. The interval $(1/2,3/2)$ is open but not closed. The whole space $X$ is both. $\blacksquare$
