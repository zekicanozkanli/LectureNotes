
> [!ex] [Exercise 39 (Euler Characteristic of Punctured Surfaces)](Exercise%2039%20%28Euler%20Characteristic%20of%20Punctured%20Surfaces%29.md): Worksheet B10. Compute $\chi$ of a sphere with the interiors of three disjoint disks removed, and of a torus with the interiors of two disjoint disks removed.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Removing each open disk lowers $\chi$ by $1$. The sphere gives $2-3=-1$; the torus gives $0-2=-2$. $\blacksquare$
