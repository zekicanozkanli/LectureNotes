
> [!ex] [Exercise 64 (Checking an Impossible Betti Number)](Exercise%2064%20%28Checking%20an%20Impossible%20Betti%20Number%29.md): Worksheet B35. A connected $2$-dimensional complex has $n=(7,15,9)$ and $b_2=0$. Find $b_1$ and explain why $b_1=2$ is impossible. Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The simplex count gives $\chi=7-15+9=1$. Thus $b_1=b_0+b_2-\chi=1+0-1=0$. The proposed value $2$ would yield $b_0-b_1+b_2=-1\ne\chi$. $\blacksquare$
