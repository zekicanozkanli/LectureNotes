
> [!ex] [Exercise 70 (Euler Characteristic of Connected Sum)](Exercise%2070%20%28Euler%20Characteristic%20of%20Connected%20Sum%29.md): Worksheet C5. Prove $\chi(S_1\#S_2)=\chi(S_1)+\chi(S_2)-2$.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Triangulate both surfaces, remove one triangle interior from each, and glue the boundary triangles. The removals lower $F$ by two. The gluing identifies three pairs of vertices and three pairs of edges, decreasing $V$ and $E$ by the same amount. Thus $V-E+F$ drops by two from the sum. $\blacksquare$
