
> [!ex] [Exercise 11 (Metric Spaces Are Hausdorff)](Exercise%2011%20%28Metric%20Spaces%20Are%20Hausdorff%29.md): Worksheet A2. Decide whether every metric space is Hausdorff.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* **True.** If $x\ne y$, set $r=d(x,y)/2$. A common point $z$ in $B_r(x)$ and $B_r(y)$ would give $d(x,y)\leq d(x,z)+d(z,y)<2r=d(x,y)$. $\blacksquare$
