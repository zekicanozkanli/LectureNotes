
> [!ex] [Exercise 46 (Tetrahedron and Its One-Skeleton)](Exercise%2046%20%28Tetrahedron%20and%20Its%20One-Skeleton%29.md): Worksheet B17. List the simplices of $\Delta^3$, verify $\chi(\Delta^3)=1$, and find the Euler characteristic of its $1$-skeleton.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The supplied count lists four vertices, six edges, four triangular faces and one tetrahedron, giving $\chi=4-6+4-1=1$. The $1$-skeleton has $\chi=4-6=-2$. The key gives the counts; the full simplex consists of all nonempty subsets of its four vertices. $\blacksquare$
