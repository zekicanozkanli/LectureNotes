
> [!ex] [Exercise 73 (Star Subcomplex Necessity)](Exercise%2073%20%28Star%20Subcomplex%20Necessity%29.md): Worksheet C8. Prove that the star of $\sigma$ can be a subcomplex only if $\sigma$ is an isolated vertex.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* Every nonempty face of $\sigma\in\operatorname{st}(\sigma)$ must contain $\sigma$ if the star is a subcomplex. Thus $\sigma$ has no proper nonempty face, so $\sigma=\{v\}$. If $v$ lay in a larger simplex $\tau$, then $\tau\setminus\{v\}$ would be a face of a star simplex but would not belong to the star. $\blacksquare$
