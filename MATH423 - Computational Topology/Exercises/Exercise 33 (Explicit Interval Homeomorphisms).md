###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* For the first map take $t\mapsto1/t$ with inverse $s\mapsto1/s$. For the second take $t\mapsto t/(1-\lvert t\rvert)$ with inverse $s\mapsto s/(1+\lvert s\rvert)$. $\blacksquare$
