
> [!ex] [Exercise 31 (Topologies on Three Points)](Exercise%2031%20%28Topologies%20on%20Three%20Points%29.md): Worksheet B2. For $X=\{a,b,c\}$, determine which collections are topologies and list their closed sets: $\mathcal{T}_1=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{b,c\},X\}$, $\mathcal{T}_2=\{\emptyset,\{a\},\{b\},\{a,b\},X\}$, and $\mathcal{T}_3=\{\emptyset,\{a,b\},\{b,c\},X\}$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* For $\mathcal{T}_1$, all four sets are both open and closed, so the closed sets are $X,\{b,c\},\{a\},\emptyset$. For $\mathcal{T}_2$, the closed sets are $X,\{b,c\},\{a,c\},\{c\},\emptyset$. The third family is not a topology because $\{a,b\}\cap\{b,c\}=\{b\}\notin\mathcal{T}_3$. $\blacksquare$
