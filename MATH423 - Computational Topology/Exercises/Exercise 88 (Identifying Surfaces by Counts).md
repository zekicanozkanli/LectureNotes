
> [!ex] [Exercise 88 (Identifying Surfaces by Counts)](Exercise%2088%20%28Identifying%20Surfaces%20by%20Counts%29.md): Lecture exercise 4. Identify a closed connected surface triangulated with $12$ vertices and $30$ edges. What if it has $10$ vertices and $30$ edges?

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* With $12$ vertices the supplied answer is $20$ triangles, $\chi=2$, and the sphere. With $10$ vertices it is $20$ triangles, $\chi=0$, and either the torus or the Klein bottle, with $b=(1,2,1)$ over $\mathbb{Z}/2$. $\blacksquare$
