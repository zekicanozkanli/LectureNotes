
> [!ex] [Exercise 51 (Counting Simplices in Dense Rips)](Exercise%2051%20%28Counting%20Simplices%20in%20Dense%20Rips%29.md): Worksheet B22. If $X$ has $N$ points at pairwise distances at most $2\epsilon$, count the simplices in $\operatorname{VR}_\epsilon(X)$. How many triangles are there for $N=100$?

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Every nonempty subset is present, giving $2^N-1$ simplices. There are $\binom{100}{3}=161{,}700$ triangles for $N=100$. $\blacksquare$
