
> [!ex] [Exercise 47 (Nerve of Four Circle Arcs)](Exercise%2047%20%28Nerve%20of%20Four%20Circle%20Arcs%29.md): Worksheet B18. Cover $S^1$ by four closed arcs, each overlapping only its two neighbors. Describe the nerve and compare its homology with $S^1$. Use coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}/2$.

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* The nerve has four vertices and four edges in a cycle; no three arcs meet, so it has no triangles. All nonempty intersections are contractible arcs. The Nerve Theorem gives the same Betti tuple $(1,1)$ as $S^1$. $\blacksquare$
