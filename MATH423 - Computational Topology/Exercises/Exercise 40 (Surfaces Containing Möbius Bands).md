
> [!ex] [Exercise 40 (Surfaces Containing Möbius Bands)](Exercise%2040%20%28Surfaces%20Containing%20M%C3%B6bius%20Bands%29.md): Worksheet B11. Which of $S^2,T^2,\mathbb{RP}^{2}$, the Klein bottle, and $T^2\#T^2$ contain a Möbius band?

###### Solution.
> [!sol] *Solution.* Precisely the nonorientable surfaces in the list do: $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ and the Klein bottle. The sphere, torus, and genus-$2$ orientable surface do not. $\blacksquare$
