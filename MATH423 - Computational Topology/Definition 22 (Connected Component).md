
> [!def] [Definition 22 (Connected Component)](Definition%2022%20%28Connected%20Component%29.md): The **connected components** of a space are its maximal connected subsets.

> [!rem] The number of connected components is a topological invariant. In Week 5 it reappears as the rank of the zeroth homology group.
