
> [!def] [[Definition 5 (Compactness)]]: A space $X$ is **compact** if every open cover has a finite subcover.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The interval $(0,1)$ is not compact. The sets $U_n=(1/n,1)$, $n\geq2$, cover it, but a finite subcollection has union $U_N=(1/N,1)$ for $N=\max n_i$, missing $1/(2N)$.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: The cover $\{(-n,n)\}_{n\geq1}$ of $\mathbb{R}$ has no finite subcover, so $\mathbb{R}$ is not compact.

> [!eg] Ex. 3: Every finite space is compact.

> [!eg] Ex. 4: By [Theorem 1 (Heine-Borel)](Theorem%201%20%28Heine-Borel%29.md), $[0,1]$, spheres $S^n$, closed balls, and polyhedra $|K|$ of finite simplicial complexes are compact.
