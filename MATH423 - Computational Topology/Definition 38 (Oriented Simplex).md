
> [!def] [Definition 38 (Oriented Simplex)](Definition%2038%20%28Oriented%20Simplex%29.md): An **orientation** of a $k$-simplex for $k\geq1$ is an ordering of its vertices up to even permutation. There are two orientations; $[v_0,\ldots,v_k]$ denotes the chosen one and $-[v_0,\ldots,v_k]$ the opposite one.

> [!rem] Computations usually choose the increasing order of vertex labels for each simplex. This is the orientation convention for the lecture’s integer coefficient section, which is outside Midterm 1.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: $[v_0,v_1]=-[v_1,v_0]$ and $[v_0,v_1,v_2]=[v_1,v_2,v_0]=-[v_1,v_0,v_2]$.
