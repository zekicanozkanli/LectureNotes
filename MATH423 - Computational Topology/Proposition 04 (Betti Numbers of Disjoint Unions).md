
> [!prop] [Proposition 04 (Betti Numbers of Disjoint Unions)](Proposition%2004%20%28Betti%20Numbers%20of%20Disjoint%20Unions%29.md): For finite simplicial complexes $K,L$ over $\mathbb{Z}/2$, $b_k(K\sqcup L)=b_k(K)+b_k(L)$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: Betti numbers add over disjoint unions (see [Exercise 78 (Betti Numbers of Disjoint Unions)](Exercises/Exercise%2078%20%28Betti%20Numbers%20of%20Disjoint%20Unions%29.md)).

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* The chain groups are direct sums and the boundary matrices are block diagonal. Ranks, cycle dimensions and boundary dimensions add, so the Betti numbers add. $\blacksquare$
