
> [!def] [Definition 41 (Simplicial Map)](Definition%2041%20%28Simplicial%20Map%29.md): A **simplicial map** $f:K\to L$ is a map on vertices sending every simplex of $K$ to a simplex of $L$, possibly of lower dimension. The induced chain map $f_{\#}$ sends a $k$-simplex to its image if the image still has dimension $k$, and to $0$ otherwise.
