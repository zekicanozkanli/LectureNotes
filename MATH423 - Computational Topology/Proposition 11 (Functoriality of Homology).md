
> [!prop] [Proposition 11 (Functoriality of Homology)](Proposition%2011%20%28Functoriality%20of%20Homology%29.md): The chain map of a simplicial map $f:K\to L$ commutes with the boundary maps, $\partial f_{\#}=f_{\#}\partial$. It induces $f_*:H_k(K)\to H_k(L)$ by $f_*[z]=[f_{\#}z]$, with $(g\circ f)_*=g_*\circ f_*$ and $(\operatorname{id})_*=\operatorname{id}$. Continuous maps of polyhedra also induce maps with these properties by simplicial approximation.

> [!rem] Commutation with the boundary maps sends cycles to cycles and boundaries to boundaries, making the map on quotient classes well defined.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: A subcomplex inclusion $L\subseteq K$ induces $H_k(L)\to H_k(K)$. In persistent homology such maps follow classes as the scale increases.
