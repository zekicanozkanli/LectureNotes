
> [!def] [Definition 24 (Orientability)](Definition%2024%20%28Orientability%29.md): A surface is **non-orientable** if it contains a subspace homeomorphic to the Möbius band; otherwise it is **orientable**.

> [!rem] A surface presented by a single polygon with paired edges is non-orientable if and only if some letter occurs twice with the same sign.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The sphere and torus are orientable. The Klein bottle and real projective plane are non-orientable.

> [!eg] Ex. 2: For the projective-plane word $abab$, a thin vertical strip between the bottom and top edges labelled $a$ glues with a half-twist: the bottom arrow points right and the top arrow left, so the left bottom side glues to the right top side. This produces a Möbius band. In the torus word $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$ the arrows agree and the strip glues without a twist, producing a cylinder.

> [!eg] Ex. 3: The cylinder and Möbius band both have $\chi=0$, but differ in orientability and in the number of boundary circles.
