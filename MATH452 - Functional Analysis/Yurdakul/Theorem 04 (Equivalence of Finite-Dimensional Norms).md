
> [!thm] [Theorem 04 (Equivalence of Finite-Dimensional Norms)](Theorem%2004%20%28Equivalence%20of%20Finite-Dimensional%20Norms%29.md) — All norms on $\mathbb{K}^{k}$, for $k\geq1$, are equivalent. In particular, on $\mathbb{K}$ all norms are equivalent.

> [!rem] For $1\leq p<\infty$ and $x=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{k})\in\mathbb{K}^{k}$, the lecture uses
>
> $$
> \|x\|_{p}=\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}|x_{i}|^{p}\right)^{1/p},\qquad
> \|x\|_{\infty}=\max_{1\leq i\leq k}|x_{i}|,
> $$
>
> with $\|x\|_{\infty}\leq\|x\|_{p}\leq k\|x\|_{\infty}$.
