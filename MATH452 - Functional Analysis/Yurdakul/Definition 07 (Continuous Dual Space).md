
> [!def] [Definition 07 (Continuous Dual Space)](Definition%2007%20%28Continuous%20Dual%20Space%29.md) — The **continuous dual space** of a normed linear space $E$ over $\mathbb{K}$ is $E'=\mathcal{L}(E,\mathbb{K})$, equipped with the operator norm.
