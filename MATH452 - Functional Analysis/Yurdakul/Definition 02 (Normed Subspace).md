
> [!def] [Definition 02 (Normed Subspace)](Definition%2002%20%28Normed%20Subspace%29.md): If $F$ is a linear subspace of a normed linear space $(E,\|\cdot\|)$, then $F$ with the restriction $\|\cdot\||_{F}$ is a **normed subspace** of $E$.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1: The space $c_{0}$ of scalar sequences converging to $0$, with $\|x\|_{\infty}=\sup_{n\in\mathbb{N}}|x_{n}|$, is a normed subspace of $(\ell_{\infty},\|\cdot\|_{\infty})$.
