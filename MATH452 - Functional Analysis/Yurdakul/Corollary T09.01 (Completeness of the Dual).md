
> [!cor] [Corollary T09.01 (Completeness of the Dual)](Corollary%20T09.01%20%28Completeness%20of%20the%20Dual%29.md) — The continuous dual $E'$ of every normed linear space $E$ is Banach.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* The scalar field $\mathbb{K}$ is Banach. Apply [Theorem 09 (Completeness of Operator Spaces)](Theorem%2009%20%28Completeness%20of%20Operator%20Spaces%29.md) with $F=\mathbb{K}$ and use $E'=\mathcal{L}(E,\mathbb{K})$. $\blacksquare$
