
> [!thm] [Theorem 03 (Continuity of Vector Operations)](Theorem%2003%20%28Continuity%20of%20Vector%20Operations%29.md) — In a normed linear space $E$, addition $A\colon E\times E\to E$, $A(x,y)=x+y$, and scalar multiplication $M\colon\mathbb{K}\times E\to E$, $M(\lambda,x)=\lambda x$, are continuous.

###### Proof.
> [!pf] *Proof.* For addition, let $(x_{n},y_{n})\to(x_{0},y_{0})$ coordinatewise. Then
>
> $$
> \|A(x_{n},y_{n})-A(x_{0},y_{0})\|
> \leq\|x_{n}-x_{0}\|+\|y_{n}-y_{0}\|\longrightarrow0.
> $$
>
> This proves continuity of addition. The scalar-multiplication argument is {unresolved} in the supplied pages.
