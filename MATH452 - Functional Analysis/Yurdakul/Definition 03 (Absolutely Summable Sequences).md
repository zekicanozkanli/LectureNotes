
> [!def] [Definition 03 (Absolutely Summable Sequences)](Definition%2003%20%28Absolutely%20Summable%20Sequences%29.md): The space $\ell_{1}$ consists of all **absolutely summable sequences** of scalars:
>
> $$
> \ell_{1}=\left\{(x_{n})_{n\geq 1}\in\mathbb{K}^{\mathbb{N}}:\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|x_{n}|<\infty\right\}.
> $$
>

###### Dependencies.
- Convergence of a Series {unresolved}
