
> [!def] [Definition 04 (Banach Space)](Definition%2004%20%28Banach%20Space%29.md) — A **Banach space** is a normed linear space that is complete for its induced metric.

###### Examples.
> [!eg] Ex. 1. For $1\leq p<\infty$, $\ell_{p}$ is a Banach space (see [Exercise 03 (Completeness of Sequence Spaces)](Exercises/Exercise%2003%20%28Completeness%20of%20Sequence%20Spaces%29.md)).
