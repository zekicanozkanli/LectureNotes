
> [!def] [Definition 06 (Operator Space and Norm)](Definition%2006%20%28Operator%20Space%20and%20Norm%29.md) — For normed linear spaces $E$ and $F$, $\mathcal{L}(E,F)$ denotes the vector space of all bounded linear operators from $E$ into $F$, with pointwise addition and scalar multiplication. The **operator norm** of $T\in\mathcal{L}(E,F)$ is
>
> $$
> \|T\|=\inf\left\{M>0:\|T(x)\|_{F}\leq M\|x\|_{E}\text{ for every }x\in E\right\}.
> $$
>

> [!rem] The defining estimate holds with the operator norm itself, $\|T(x)\|_{F}\leq\|T\|\|x\|_{E}$ for every $x\in E$.
